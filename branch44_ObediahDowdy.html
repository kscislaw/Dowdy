Obediah Dowdy
Branch #44 - Cumberland Co and Nottoway Co VA

This Lineage Last Reviewed on September 2023
Obediah Dowdy of Cumberland Co and Nottoway Co Virginia born say 1766 likely in Cumberland Co VA where he appeared on a personal property tax list over the age of 21 in 1787. He married Mary Davenport in Cumberland Co in 1789; After marriage, he appeared in Amelia Co in the household of Mary Goode. In 1797 he purchased 115 acres of land in Nottoway Co VA where he died sometime before 1806 when his estate inventory was probated in that county.

1787 Cumberland County VA Personal Property Tax List - Daniel Allen - Name of wm tithable +21: Himself and Obediah Dowdy; 0 wm 16-21; 9 blacks +16, 8 blacks -16; 6 horses; 29 cattle

1790 Amelia Co VA Personal Tythable List - Obey Dawdy (Mary Goode charged with tax)

1797 February 2 Nottoway Co DB 1 pg 584 - Obediah Dowdy from Milner Bennatt - Miliner Bennett of Nottoway Co of 1st part and Obediah Dowdy of same co of 2nd part L130. sold to said Obediah Dowdey a tract of land lying in county aforesaid on both sides of horse pen creek bounded by lines of Wm Carter's , James Borum's, Elas Dyernett's, Stephen Dyernett's, Lemuel Cockes' and Rawleigh Carters containign 115 acres <s> Milner Bennett Wit Joseph J Fowlkes, Wm Walton, Cassemer Cabaniss. Acknowledged in Nottoway court 2 February 1797.

1806 October 9 - Bedford Co WB 2 pg 469 - Inventory and appraisment of Obediah Dowdy dec'd estate viz: 1 negro woman named Lid L30; same Rose L110; same Biddy L55; same- Ursley L45; same (not named) L30; [list of appraised livestock, carts, household items] Agreeable to an order from Nottoway Co court to us directed we proceeded to appraise the personal estate of Obediah Dowdy dec'd as above 29 Oct 1806 Elias E Carter, Elias Byernatt, Bowler Byernatt

1845 July 3 Campbell Co WB 9 pg 414 - Agreeable to an order of the county court of Campbell made at June term 1845 appointing as commissioners to appraise the estate of Obediah Dowdy dec'd we have this day appraised the following property showed to us by Mr Leonard Phillips: Rose a negro woman slave aged 70 years $0.00; Suckey a negroe woman slave age 25 years $400.00, Newry a boy slave aged 4 years $180.00, John eighteen months, a slave $100.00. Total $680.00; Appraised by Zahariah Phillips, Benjamin Ward, Thomas H Burras and Jas Carson on 3 July 1845 Note it is not unusual to wait to perform a final inventory of a decedant's estate until his widow finally dies, often many years later. This is to distribute her "dower" part of husband's estate to her children
