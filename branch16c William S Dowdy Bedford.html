William Dowdy
Branch #16c - Bedford Co VA

This Lineage Last Reviewed and Updated September 2023
William S. Dowdy of Bedford Co Virginia Not enumerated in 1840 census, likely living with a parent prior to his marriage later that year. He died sometime prior to December 1847 when his estate was inventoried and sold in Bedford Co VA

1844 December 21 - Bedford Co DB 31 pg 253 - Elijah Drewry of Bedford Co of 1st part. William S Dowdy of sd county of 2nd part. David H Creasey of Campbell Co of 3rd part. Elijah Drewry is indebted to David H Creasy $30 by date 1 Jan 1843. Bargained and sold unto William S Dowdy the following property [household items, horse, tobacco etc] should Drewry default on the bond to David H Creasey <mark> Elijah Drewry, Wm S Dowdy. Proven 2 December 1844

1847 December 1 Bedford County Will Book pg 523 - Sales of the estate of William S Dowdy dec'd by Micajah Davis D. S. for Armistead Otey Sheriff of Bedford Co and as such inv on the 1st day Dec 1847 upon a credit of 12 months for all [ ] over $5. [buyers included] Richard Foster, John Creasey, Pleasant Stinnell? William B Overstreet, Walter B Dowdy, Benj Scott, Peter Weeks, Pleasant Bonds, Granville R Sullin?, Thomas Cheek, Griffin A Dobyns, Wm Shepherd, George Scott, John B Witt, Daniel B Stevens, John Drury, David H Creasy, and Mary A Dowdy [by far the largest buyer]1847 December 27 - At a court held for Bedford County - The list of sales of the estate of William S Dowdy dec'd was produced in court and ordered to be recorded

married Mary Ann Scott by bond dated 23 November 1840 at Bedford Co VA; She was the daughter of Nancy Scott; She was widowed with children living in Bedford Co VA both in 1850 and 1860 censuses.

Bedford Co VA Marriage Bonds - Nov. 23, 1840; William S. Dowdy & Mary Ann Scott, dt Nancy; David H. Creasey, Surety; Married by James Leftwich, Nov. 26, 1840.

children:

1(i). James F Dowdy born about 1843 VA, age 7 in widowed mother's 1850 census household; Age 17 in mother's 1860 census household

1(ii). Mary Jane Dowdy born about 1844 VA, age 6 in widowed mother's 1850 census household; Age 16 in mother's 1860 census household (as M. J.)

1(iii). Ann W Dowdy born about 1846 VA, age 4 in widowed mother's 1850 census household; Age 13 in mother's 1860 census household (as A. W.)
