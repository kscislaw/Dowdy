Nicholas Dowdy
Branch #23

Nicholas Dowdy of Chatham NC, Virginia and Greene Co MO born about 1825 NC. Married and enumerated at Upper Regiment, Chatham Co NC in 1850 census, age 24 b NC; Nicholas served in the Confederate army but defected the NC Troops on 12 February 1865 near St. Petersburg, VA; Nicholas Dowdy's service record indicates at the time of his desertion he had not been paid any wages since his enlistment 24 October 1864. He defected on the same day/place as a John Dowdy also from Chatham, NC. After giving birth to all his children in North Carolina, he and wife Lilly moved to Carroll County VA sometime before the 1870 census. The family were enumerated at Sulphur Springs, Carroll Co VA in 1870 census, Nicholas age 45 'works on farm', also in Carroll Co VA in 1880 census, age 55, farmer born NC (parents born NC). Moved to Greene County, Springfield, Missouri around 1883

married Lillie Ann Edwards; She was born about 1825 NC, age 55 in 1880 census household
