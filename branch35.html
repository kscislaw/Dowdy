James C. Dowdy of Whitfield Co GA born about 1815 in Jackson Co Georgia (1864 Militia list); Enumerated in Floyd Co GA in 1840, age 20-30; Not found in 1850 census (as yet); Enumerated at Gordon Co GA in 1860, age 45, age 55 in Whitfield Co GA census of 1870; He died after 1880 in Whitfield County GA.

"Muster Roll of the Guard organized for the Security, relief and protection of the citizens and Friendly Cherokees resident in the Cherokee Territory in the limits of the State of Georgia Commanded by William N. Bishop from the first day of July when Mustered into Service to the 13th of September 1835, inclusive: Wilson R. Young, 1st Sergt., James Sample, 2nd Mas. Sergt, James M. Kelly, Fifer, Hardy Burwell, drummer. Private: James C. Dowdy." - Whites Among the Cherokees (book)

James C. Dowdy is on Payroll record from Oct. 1, 1835 through November 30, 1835 Source Military Records page 3131, GA Archives.

1840 Floyd Co GA Census - J Dowdy 1000100000000 - 0001000000000 (age 20-30, b 1810-1820)

Robert Dowdy, age 16 yrs 6 mos. farmer b. Floyd Co GA

Note: Several Dowdys are found in Jackson Co GA during 1815 period; Robert, William, Benjamin Dowdy; Also, a William Dowdy is found in Gordan Co court records at same time as James C Dowdy (not sure if this is significant)
