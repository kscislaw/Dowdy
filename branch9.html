James Dowdy Sr.
Branch #9

This Lineage Last Reviewed and Updated August 2023
James Dowdy Sr of Moore Co NC born 20 April 1779; Enumerated in Moore Co NC in 1800, 1810, 1820, 1830, 1840, 1850, age 71, and in 1860 censuses, age 81; He was a member of the Friendship Baptist Church in Moore Co as well as the Bethlehem Mission Baptist Church; He died 25 December 1868 Moore NC (tombstone inscription); A few records of his estate settlement exist in extant Moore Co NC records c 1870, naming payments to various heirs and presumed heirs, son Archibald Dowdy administrator; He was buried at Dowdy Cemetery, Moore Co NC; Note: by proximity, James Dowdy Sr may have been the son of Daniel Dowdy of Chatham, later Moore Co NC (who then apparently migrated to North Georgia). No evidence other than geography as yet.
