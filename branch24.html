George K. Dowdy
Branch #24 - Stoddard Co Missouri

This Lineage Last Reviewed and Updated September 2023
George K Dowdy of Stoddard Co MO born about 1772, He moved to Kentucky abt 1810 then to Tennessee by 1814. Before his death, George K. and his sons bought and sold large pieces of property in Hickman and Fulton Cos., KY, Obion and Lake Cos., TN and New Madrid and Stoddard Cos., MO. this included islands in the Mississippi. By 1840 George K. joined Allen Dowdy (possibly his brother) in Stoddard Co., MO. He died there in 1852. "

It's very tempting to assign George K Dowdy as the son of William Dowdy who died and left probate records in the early 1800s in Chatham Co NC. However, something bothers me. George's son Wm C Dowdy married the daughter of William Dowdy of Stewart Co TN. In the old days, they were likely cousins, probably 1st cousins. The problem is that William Dowdy of Stewart Co TN was born in SC, his children born in SC. That means he originated out of the Dowdys who migrated from Chatham Co NC to the Cheraw/Chesterfield/Lancaster Co SC area. And the children of these heads of households who appear in SC census records (including one named George Dowdy) are pretty much all unknown. Thus, George K Dowdy might also be from these SC families - although, his own children were born in NC, not SC - thus conflicting signals.
