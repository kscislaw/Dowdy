This exercise categorizes all available land records posted on this website for Chatham Co NC as located either in the Deep River Area (southern Chatham) or the Rocky River Area (northern Chatham). All references to Daniel Dowdy of Deep River and a few others are removed to concentrate on identifying the Thomas and William Dowdys of Chatham Co.

Thomas Dowdy, Senior, Yeoman of Chatham Co. NC

The following is evidence that the father of Thomas Dowdy of Albemarle Parish was Thomas Dowdy, Senior, yeoman of Orange/Chatham NC

1. Please notice the 1789 abstract (above) where Thomas Dowdy, Junior, planter of Anson NC sells land he inherited from his deceased brother William, to a Thomas Dowdy, Senior, yeoman of Chatham NC. This abstract makes a few things clear

William, the brother of Thomas Dowdy "junior" is DEAD. This fact eliminates Thomas Dowdy b.1759 (Albemarle P.) as the seller of the land. His brother William is alive in 1789. However, his father, Thomas Dowdy b abt 1730 (father of Albemarle Parish children) COULD be "junior".

Thomas Dowdy, "junior", the seller of the land, is the SOLE legatee of his brother William. In other words, his father only had two sons. Again, that eliminates Thomas Dowdy b 1759 as the seller.

If Thomas Dowdy b 1759 is not "junior" (in the 1789 abstract), then who is? Of course, at this point, it is important to say that "junior" does not necessarily mean the "son of Thomas Dowdy, Senior". But the following evidence suggests it to be so. (the following from Betty Moran)

2. If you track the land owned by Thomas Dowdy b abt 1730 (father of the children in Albemarle Parish VA), around Nicks and Mudlick Creeks, you discover that he is known as JUNIOR and that he inherited land from his father Thomas Dowdy, SENIOR.

Thomas Dowdy buys 50 acres from Joseph Pinson in 1763. This is more likely Thomas SENIOR because "Junior" is still in Virginia....but it doesn't matter which one it is. TOTAL: Thomas Sr b.abt 1710- 50 acres, Thomas Jr b. abt 1730 - 0 acres

Thomas Dowdy got a 300 acre grant on Mudlick Creek, but sold 90 acres to Thomas Ray.  TOTAL: Thomas Sr- 50 acres, Thomas Jr- 210 acres

B. Then Thomas Dowdy JR (of Anson/Albemarle Parish) inherited land on Nicks Crk from deceased brother William, sold 112 acres to Thomas Dowdy SR, and kept the other 100.  TOTAL: Thomas Sr.-162 acres, Thomas Jr.- 310 acres

C. Then Thomas Dowdy Jr of Anson/Albemarle got a 150 acre grant on Nicks Creek. TOTAL: Thomas Sr.- 162 acres, Thomas Jr.- 460 acres

D. Then when SR died, Thomas JR (b. abt 1730 of Anson/Albemarle), as only surviving son got back the 112 acres on Nicks creek that he had sold to SR in 1789, and now he has land on both creeks. Making a total of 572 acres that probably included all the land between Nicks Creek and Mudlick creek, down to Rocky River. TOTAL: Thomas Dowdy Sr- (dead), Thomas Dowdy Jr.- 622 acres (162 via inheritance)

E. Thomas Dowdy JR  b 1730 (of Anson/father of Albemarle) gave to children: 44 acres and 56 acres to Bartley, 100 acres to Sarah, 100 acres to Anne, 100 acres to granddaughter Rachel, a total of 400 acres, leaving him owning 222 acres at the time of his death in 1802, which was divided 7 ways between his 7 children (son John was not listed. Is he the John that died in 1792?). TOTAL: Thomas Dowdy Jr - 222 acres

F. Sarah Dowdy and husband Wm. Norman kept their 1/7 share , and bought the other seven 1/7 shares from the other 6 children in the deed dated 1806 which says they sold 182 acres to Wm. Norman. But this 182 acres was only 6/7 of the total, which would make each share 30.2 acres. So if the Normans kept their 30.2 acres, and bought 182 acres from the others, the total would have been 212 acres. [NOTE: After using "Plat Drawing" software, the "heirs" plat totaled 218.5 acres, not 212, a number much closer to the 222 neeeded to completely sell Thomas Dowdy's remaining land] TOTAL: Thomas Dowdy Jr. - approx 0 acres.

Remember, Thomas Dowdy b 1730 (father of the children in Albemarle P.) needs approximately 222 acres of additional land (from somewhere) to fulfill all of his land gifts to his children. He is short that amount from his land grants and land purchases. The 1789 abstract providing 212 acres inherited from "brother" William Dowdy IN THE RIGHT AREA OF CHATHAM...fits perfectly.

THEREFORE, THE FATHER OF THOMAS DOWDY (b abt 1730 father of the children in Albemarle Parish VA) WAS THOMAS DOWDY, SENIOR

Since there are only TWO early Dowdys in Orange/Chatham NC, William Dowdy and Thomas Dowdy (both found in early road orders), it is probably safe to assume that the early Thomas Dowdy found in early Orange NC is the "Thomas Dowdy, Senior, yeoman", father of Thomas Dowdy b abt 1730 (father of the children in Albemarle Parish VA).
