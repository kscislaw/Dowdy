Administrations

1754 John Dowdey estate. Administration to Eliza Dowdey, widow of dec'd

July 2 1754 Court. Eliza Dowdy, widow of John Dowdy moves to have letters of administration on estate of John Dowdey, as relict of the dec'd. Approved.

1754 November 16 Onslow Co Estate Records [loose] - A true inventory of all and singular the goods chattles and creditts of John Dowdey late deceased that hath come to my hands possession or knowledge on the 16th of Nov 1754 as follows: [list of goods] 2 horses, 1 gun, 14 head of hogs, 1 canoe, etc etc [notes:] McCray, John Jarreth, Edmond Howard etc etc. A true inventory returned this 16th of Nov 1754 by me <mark> Eliz Dowdey
