1847 Iredell Co Deed Book - This Indenture made this fifth day of March One thousand Eight Hundred & forty seven between James Dowdy of the State of North Caroliner of the one part & John Crawford of the County & State Aforesaid of the other part . Witnesseth that the said James Dowdy owing to losses & Misfortune is at present unable to discharge his just debts - & is willing to assign all of his property for the benefit of his creditors - Now this Indenture - Witness Seth that the said James Dowdy , as well in consider cation of the premises & for the purpose of making a just distribution of his Estate & effects among his creditor - as also the sum of one Dollar . to him in hand paid by the said John Crawford....Eighty five acres more or less adjoining the lands of the Widow Campbell & others - one horse & one one horse Waggon fifteen head of Hoys give head of Cattle - all of his household & Kitchen furniture. <s> James Dowdy Wit: James McClellan

1883 Feb 9 - State of North Carolina , Iredel IN THE MATTER OF Mary Ann Dowdy Widow , & c. The undersigned , E B Stinson Justice of the Peace , --- and Daniel Freeze and J B Patterson Commissioners , duly summoned and sworn , do hereby assign and allot to Mary A Dowdy widow of James Dowdy deceased , the following articles of personal property of the value annexed , 90 Valued at 40 " Com 50 175 the Bacon , green , 10 saw & 3 pigs 5 Cattle 14 Sheep All the Roughness on hand house 1 horse Waggon 4 Stands Bus All the farming tools on hand Hanschote, Kitchen & Table furniture on hands 2 Rifle guns & 1 musket Mechanic tools on hand We also find , upon examination , that the number of the family of the said widow , exclusive of herself is none - There being a deficiency of the articles on hand to make up the Year's Support , we assess such deficiency at Fifty Five 60 /100 Dollars to be paid by the personal representative . This 90 day of February 1883 . E. B Stimson J. P Dan Freeze B. Patterson

Heritage of Iredell Co Vol 1 page 268 [biography #153] - DOWDY FAMILY - "The name was originally Dowda, but sometime in the late 1800s family members began spelling it Dowdy. James Anderson Dowdy was born on Feb 7, 1815. He was the son of Frances Anderson and Edward Dowda of Fairfax VA. Edward Dowda may have immigrated from Holland or Germany......"
