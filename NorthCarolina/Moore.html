Moore County
North Carolina

Note: Various tax lists for Moore Co are extant beginning 1790s, thru about 1850. But all but the 1815 and 1818/23 lists are partiial, some lists in the 1840s represent mulitple districts, but none cover areas where Dowdys lived (no Dowdys on lists). But later lists beginning about 1850 in possession of historical society contain the correct district for Dowdys

1836, Mar 18 -- Deed Book 43 Page 372, Moore County, NC - James Dowdy deeded Samuel McIntosh 50 acres located northwest of Richland Creek adjoining Jesse Ritter, Campbell and Nathan Smith. Wm. J. McIntosh and Elizabeth Cox were witnesses.

1837, Oct 21 -- A History of Sandy Creek Baptist Association by George Purefoy, Page 120 - James Dowdy, Leonard Lawhorn and D. Bryant from Bethlehem Church (Moore County) were delegates to the Association meeting at Mineral Spring Meeting House in Chatham County, NC.

1838, Jul 29 -- Bethlehem Baptist Church Minutes, Carthage, NC Page 26 "Bro. Richardson complained against James Dowdy for Bastardy and living in an adulterous way which was immediately taken up and Dowdy excluded for the same out of our fellowship."

1839, Jan 29 -- Deed Book 82 Page 53, Moore County, NC - James Dowdy Sr. deeded James Dowdy Jr. tracts [1] 41 acres located on Locust Branch being part of Donald McQueen's 100 acre grant [2] 9 acres adjoining Stephen Davis and Jesse Ritter. Artemus Shattuck was a witness.

The Eagle (Fayetteville) 28 April 1870, pg 3 - Arch'd B Dowdy adm'r of James Dowdy vs William Dowdy and others. Petition for account and settlement. In the above case it appearing to the court that William Dowdy, Heirs of Thomas Dowdy, John Dowdy, Benjamin Dowdy; Heirs of of Sallie Upton and Bryant Caddell are defendants in this case and that they reside beyond the limits of this state. It is ordered that publication be made for 6 successive weeks in the Eagle a newspaper published in the Town of Fayetteville, notifying and summoning said defendants to appear before our said court on or before 1st June 1870 and answer fthe complaint, otherwise the prayer of plantiff will be granted. Wit: A H McNeill, clerk of said court at office in Carthage 31 March 1870.
