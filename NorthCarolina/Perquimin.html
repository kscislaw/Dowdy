Perquimin County
North Carolina
 

1732 Perquimins Quit Rent. Edward Dowdy "part" owes rent on 500 acres

1735 Perq. Court. McDaniel vs Dowdy. Edward Dowdy, carpenter, in custody of the marshall indebted required or lue thereof in carpenter work in shingling a house for Moses Spiks in the room of said plaintiff...not yet paid or work done...plt failed to appear...dismissed.

1735 Perq Deed (1736 Court) John Parish Sr to Edward Dowdy, planter of Perquimins Precinct of Albemarle County NC 500 ac adjacent Eau Albertson. Wit; Thomas Weekes, Samuel Bundy

1737/8 Perq. Deed. Edward Dowdy to Robert Hall 100 acres on Deep Creek adjacent Esau Albertson (now John Huffton), John Arnold and John Parish...his marke. Wit: Comfort Dowdy, John Huffton

1742. Perq. Administration Bonds....Comfort Dowdy, widow to Samuel Wright

1753 Perq. Court. Petition of Samuel Wright. Letters of Administration of estate of john Gordon in behalf of Jemima Dowdy, a minor. Security Thomas Weekes Esq. and John Perry.

1754 Perq. Court. Last Will and Testament of Samuel Wright. Proved by Jemima Dowdy and Isaac Sittison, two of the subscribing witnesses. Exor: Eliz. Wright and Nathaniel Welch

1762. Perq. John Nicholis and wife Jemima to James Jones part of 500 acrs patent to John Parish to Edward Dowdy (100 acres to Robert Hall) the remaining 400 acres fell to said Jemima, only daughter of said Dowdy, land near end of Turkey Ridge. Adj William Carson.
