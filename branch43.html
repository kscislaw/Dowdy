William Dowdy
Walker County, AL

This Lineage Last Reviewed and Updated September 2023
William Dowdy of Walker Co Alabama born 30 November 1801 in Georgia; He moved to Walker Co AL prior to 1838 when he patented 40 acres of land there in 1838; He was elected as Justice of the Peace of Walker County in 1846 and 1847; He was enumerated in Walker Co AL in the 1850 census, age 50 (as William Dowda); He died 11 December 1863

1840 July 1 - Lumpkin Co DB H pg 321 - Chatham Co GA - Indenture between William Dowdy of Alabama by his atty David R Myers of Muscogee Co of 1st part and Thomas Kemp of county first named (Chatham Co GA); $10; sold a tract of land to Thomas Kemp lying in Cherokee, now Lumpkin Co Lot #202 containing 40 acres <s> William Dowdy by his atty David R Myers Wit: Joh Griffith, Wm C Wylly JP, Recorded 2 April 1844

1850 Walker Co AL Census - William Dowda 50, Margret 43, Nancy 21, Margret 19, Melissa 17
