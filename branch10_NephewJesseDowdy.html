1(i). Jesse Dowdy born 1770-80; Likely the same Jesse Dowdy identified as 'nephew' in the 1804 will of James Dowdy of Cumberland Co VA He was named co-executor of that will. He was enumerated in the taxable household of uncle James Dowdy's estate in 1804, thus over the age of 21 at the time.

1804 Cumberland Co VA - Will of James Dowdy - ....My will and desire is that the child my negro woman Fanny is now pregnat with, should it be born alive be given to nephew Jesse Dowdy, which said negro Fanny is now lend to my wife, also I give to my said nephew Jesse Dowdy ten poinds to be raised out of the crops made on the plantation and by the negroes lent to my wife, all of which I give to him and his heirs forever....

1804 Cumberland Co VA Personal Property Tax List - James Dowdy est - Name of free whites +21: Jesse Dowdy, Richard Dowdy; Names of Blacks +16: Becky, Fanny, Prince, ?Patty, total 4; Names of Blacks 12-16: Mourning, total 1; 6 horses

1807 Cumberland Co VA Personal Property Tax List - Jesse Dowdy 1 free tithes, 1 blacks over 16; 0 blacks over 12-16, 2 horses

1820 Cumberland VA Census - Jesse Dowdy Sr 410001 - 21001 (age 45+, b bef 1775)

1830 Cumberland Co Census (and # slaves m, f) - Jesse Dowdy 00111001 - 0110001 (2m, 2f) (age 50-60, b 1770-80)

1840 Cumberland Co Census - Jesse Dowdy 000020001 - 000000001 (age 60-70, b 1770-80)

married Dorothea Davenport by bond dated 4 July 1804 in Cumberland Co VA; She was age 70 residing in the household of James H Dowdy (age 33) and wife Nancy in 1850 Cumberland Co VA census; She died 25 August 1855 according to the Wilkerson family bible. The bible also lists Dolly “D” Dowdy’s date of death as 1/23/1865.
