Joseph Dowdy
Branch #27

This Lineage Last Reviewed and Updated September 2023
Joseph Dowdy born about 1785 NC (1860 and 1870 census). Enumerated in Williamson Co TN in 1820 and 1830 censuses. Then migrated to Graves Co KY by the time of the 1840 census. Enumerated in Graves Co KY census for the years 1840, 1850, 1860 and 1870. He died sometime between 1870-80 at Mayfield, Graves KY
