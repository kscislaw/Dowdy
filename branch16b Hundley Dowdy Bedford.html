Bedford DB 37 pg 470 - 9 July 1855 - Hundly Dowdy Sr to Mildred T Dowdy and her children for love and affection for sd Mildred Dowdy and her children all his land it being the same wheron he now resides consisting of several contiguous tracts containing altogether 200 acres near the Dumplin [ ] and bounded by the lands of Cary A Dowdy, John Bawy..[] Overstreet at aothers. The sd Hundley Dowdy gives the said Mildred L Dowdy all his personal property of every description; <s> Hundley Dowdy; Wit: Jesse Minter, Pleasant Swain [Note: Mildred is the widow of Walter B Dowdy who died in Bedford Co VA in 1855]

married Eleanor Creasy by bond dated 5 December 1796 in Bedford Co., VA. She was the daughter of John Creasy of Bedford Co VA and the sister of Elizabeth Creasy Dowdy who married William Dowdy Sr of Bedford Co VA. She was alive and enumerated in husband's 1850 census household, age 65 (Sic, seems too young considering marriage in 1796)

Bedford Co VA Marriage Bonds - Dec. 5, 1796; Hundley Dowdy & Elenor Creasey; John Creasey, Surety; Married by Alderson Weeks, Dec. 8, 1796.

1820 August 29 - Bedford Co VA WB 6 pg 248 - Will of John Creasy ... Also to my 3 daughters Molly Wilson, Ealoner Dowdy, Elizabeth Dowdy having received their legacies except Ealoner Dowdy and Elizabeth Dowdy which is to be paid one dollar each.

children:

they had 8 daughters and 4 sons according to the 1820 census

1(i). Hundley Dowdy Jr born about 1815 in VA (age 35 in 1850 census); He was identified as son of Hunley Dowdy Sr in 1844 Franklin Co VA deed, Appeared in Franklin Co personal property taxes between 1843 and 1848. He married the widow Isabella Austin in 1844 in Franklin Co VA. He and Isabella moved back to Bedford Co VA in the 1850 census where he was enumerated there, age 35 (born 1815); The family lived at Marion, Smyth Co Virginia in 1860 census, age 45
