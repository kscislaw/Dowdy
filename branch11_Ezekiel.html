Ezekiel Dowdy born say 1766; He first appeared in Bedford VA tax records in 1787 as tithable - over age 21 - in the household of Joel Leftwich; He was taxed in Bedford Co in subsequent years through 1800; Named in the 1805 will of father John Dowdy of Bedford Co VA; He purchased 100 acres of land in Franklin Co VA in 1799 but likely migrated to Franklin Co VA about 1803 when he appeared in personal property taxes in that county; Ezekiel Dowdy was enumerated in Franklin Co VA in the 1810 and 1820 censuses; Age 60-70 in 1830 Franklin Co Census; He was alive in 1834 and 1835 when he acted as surety for 2 marriages in Franklin Co VA; He last appeared in Franklin Co personal property tax lists in 1838, likely dying about that year.

Item 2nd I give and bequeath my property consisting of 1 cow and calf and their increase, also 1 feather bed bedstead adn clothing unto George Hopkins and his wife Elizabeth and their children. Item 3rd I give and bequeath to my daughter Mary Hopkins two dollars to be paid out of the money coming from the administration of my father, also my large bible and 1 hymn book. Item 4th I give the remainder of the money coming from my father adm'r unto George Hopkins and his wife Elizabeth and their children forever. Item 5th and lastly I desire that George Hopkins shall act as my executor to this my last will and testament revoking all former wills and and testaments by me made signed and sealed by me this 9th day of January 1842 <mark> Jane Dowdy. James Dowdy born about 1799 in VA, age 50 in 1850 census; He first appeared in records as being of tithable age in 1819 Franklin County personal property tax lists; He married in Franklin County VA earlier in that year. He and wife and one daughter appeared in Franklin Co census in 1820. The family next moved to Giles County VA where James was enumerated there in 1830 and in 1840 censuses. In 1850, James was identified as age 50, farmer, born Virginia. His household included his wife Elizabeth and numerous children born after 1829. James Dowdy died sometime soon after 1850 census, documents regarding the disposition of his estate in Chancery Court are dated early 1851

married Elizabeth Wysong by bond dated 10 February 1819 in Franklin Co VA; She was the daughter of Joseph Wysong.

Feb. 10, 1819. Franklin Co VA Marriage Bonds - Dowdy, James and Elizabeth Wysong, dau. Joseph,Sur. Abraham Childress. 98. page 77
