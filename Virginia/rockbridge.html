1843 May 4 Campbell Co VA DB 25 pg 56 - Wm B Dowdy to Jas Johnston DT 4 May 1843 between William Dowdy of 1st part. James Johnston of 2nd part both of Rockbridge Co Indebted to Armistead W Forrest for the amount of $121. Wm B Dowdy hath granted unto said James Johnston all his the said Dowdys interest either at law or in equity as one of the five distributees of Obadiah Dowdy dec'd late of Nottaway Co in four slaves to wit: Rose, Susan, Mike and infant child of Susan, said slaves are now held by Mary Dowdy the widow of Obadiah Dowdy and mother of the said Wm B Dowdy which said Mary Dowdy resides now with the said slaves in the county of Campbell and holds them as her dower or distributees portion of the slaves of said Obadiah Dowdy who died intestate; If default to sell his interest; <s> Wm B Dowdy, J Johnston Acknowledged in Campbell court 10 July 1843

8 January 1857 Samuel McDaniel, 42, Widower, born Rockbridge, lives Rockbridge, Farmer, son of Saml. and Jane McDaniel, married Jane Dowdy, 27, Spinster, born Rockbridge, lives Rockbridge, daughter of Wm. and Jane Dowdy, Emmett P. Mason officiating

September 4th 1865 At Rockbridge County Court The last Will and testament of Jane Johnston deceased was produced in court, proved by the oaths of William B. Dowdy and John M. Wilson, subscribing witnesses thereto and the stamp duties thereon being paid, is ordered to be recorded.
