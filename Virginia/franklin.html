1842 January 9 - Franklin Co WB 5 pg 356 - Will of Jane Dowdy dec'd - I Jane Dowdy of the co of Franklin and state of Virginia tho affected in body tho being of sound mind and disposing memory do hereby make and ordain this my last will and testament in manner and form as follows - Item 1st I desire that all my just debts shall be paid. Item 2nd I give and bequeath my property consisting of 1 cow and calf and their increase, also 1 feather bed bedstead adn clothing unto George Hopkins and his wife Elizabeth and their children. Item 3rd I give and bequeath to my daughter Mary Hopkins two dollars to be paid out of the money coming from the administration of my father, also my large bible and 1 hymn book. Item 4th I give the remainder of the money coming from my father adm'r unto George Hopkins and his wife Elizabeth and their children forever. Item 5th and lastly I desire that George Hopkins shall act as my executor to this my last will and testament revoking all former wills and and testaments by me made signed and sealed by me this 9th day of January 1842 <mark> Jane Dowdy. Witness J Early (he is Joab Early), James Wright, Henry G Hopkins. Proved 7 March 1842
