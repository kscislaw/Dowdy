POWHATAN COUNTY VA ABSTRACTS

1777 Powhatan County formed from Cumberland Co VA

1805, 1806, 1807, 1809, 1810 Powhatan Co Personal Property Tax Lists

no Dowdys

1810 Powhatan County VA Census

Benjamin Dowdy 20210 - 20010 (age 26-45, b 1765-1784, probably young family w 2 older males in HH)

he is possibly the Benjamin Dowdy son of Richard Dowdy of Prince Edward Co VA. That Benjamin sued in Chancery there in 1828. See also Benjamin Dowdy of Amelia Co VA, whose last tax appearance was 1809

1812 Powhatan Co VA Personal Property Tax Lists

1812 May 10 Southam Parish Vestry Book pg 267 At a court of monthly sessions holden for Powhatan County done in Capt Hanry W Watkins Company district - Returns of Processioning [of Land] done in 1811 and 1812 [list of names]

When Processioned: March 4 1812; Whose lines: Benj Dowdy; Hugh French

Note: Hugh French owned a famous landmark tavern in Powhatan Co, ultimately owned 2000 acres, died intestate in 1842
