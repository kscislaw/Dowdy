ENGLAND/VIRGINIA ABSTRACT

1666 Oct 24 Chancery [England] Suit of  Thomas Dowde and Margaret his wife, only surviving sister of John Whitty of London, mariner, deceased versus Sarah, relect of John Whitty and John Currier of London, merchant. John Whitty had plantations in Carolina, Virginia, and New Jersey which Dowde alleges to have devised by will to his wife.
