Virginia
Nottoway County
Formed from Amelia County in 1789 (boundary adjusted 1792)
Many early deed, marriage and court records destroyed by Union troops

1826 July 6 Nottoway Co WB pg 379 - The estate of Ambrose Dowdy in a/c with Edward T Jeffreys [?]: 1823 May cash paid Jas B Anderson per recpt of said A Dowdy; July cash paid Jas B Anderson per recpt; Amt of J L and Hunts claim in attachments; By this amt received of Commissioners of Obadiah Dowdy dec'd etc The above account of Edward T Jeffress committee of Ambrose Dowdy was this day examined and noted by us. We certify that the transactions of the committee of said Ambrose Dowdy that there is due....John H Knight, H Hatchett, Jno B Oliver (Note: this does not say Ambrose is dec'd. he is probably was a minor orphan of Obediah Dowdy dec'd and this was an old guardian account finally being settled. See his deed next in 1828; Note father died in 1806 so 20 years earlier.)

1843 May 4 Campbell Co VA DB 25 pg 56 - Wm B Dowdy to Jas Johnston deed of trust - 4 May 1843 between William B Dowdy of 1st part. James Johnston of 2nd part both of Rockbridge Co Indebted to Armistead W Forrest for the amount of $121. Wm B Dowdy hath granted unto said James Johnston all his the said Dowdys interest either at law or in equity as one of the five distributees of Obadiah Dowdy dec'd late of Nottaway Co in four slaves to wit: Rose, Susan, Mike and infant child of Susan, said slaves are now held by Mary Dowdy the widow of Obadiah Dowdy and mother of the said Wm B Dowdy which said Mary Dowdy resides now with the said slaves in the county of Campbell and holds them as her dower or distributees portion of the slaves of said Obadiah Dowdy who died intestate; If default to sell his interest; <s> Wm B Dowdy, J Johnston Acknowledged in Campbell court 10 July 1843
