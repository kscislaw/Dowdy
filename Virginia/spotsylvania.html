Virginia
Spotsylvania County

1720s

1721 Spotsylvania Co VA formed from Essex, King William, and King and Queen Counties

1725. Spotsylvania Co Court Records - John Hawkins vs George Dowdey. Awarded Hawkins but dismissed due to Hawkins death

1727 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - Thomas Dowdey overseer of road from marks of stones to Germanna (previously served under John Finlayson in clearing same road)

1727 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - John Dowdey vs Richard Rogers. Dowdey fails to appear

1727 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - Robt Thomas vs Thomas Dowdey. Plaintiff fails to appear

1727 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - Richard Long vs Thomas Dowdey. Both failed to appear (case appears again in 1729)

1728 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - Thomas Dowdey discharded as overseer of road. Benjamin Cave appointed

1728 St George's Parish Levy. John Dowdey "Wolves Heads" 1 , "By whom granted" William Johnson gent , "Tobacco" 200

1728 September 28 - Virginia Land Patents Vol 14 pg 104 - Grant unto George Dowdy of Spotsylvania Co VA a certain tract of land containing 1000 acres lying on the branch of the North Fork of the Northanna in Co of Spotsylvania afsd bounded to wit: Andrew Harrison's Anthony Ghelson's, Coll Corbin's line

1729 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - James Taylor vs George Dowdey. Case dismissed

1729 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - John Dowdey was taken into custody in George Tilly vs David MackMurry who was security for Dowdey

1729 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - Tresspass by John Dowdey on George Tilly's land

1729 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - James Taylor gent. vs George Dowdey. Granted Taylor

1729 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - John Dowdey gets attachment on estate of Richard Lewis

1729 St George's Parish Levy. Thomas Dowdey "Wolves Heads" 1, "By whom granted" Robert Slaughter, gent, "Tobacco" 200

1729 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - John Dowdy is paid 1500 lbs tobacco in estate of Richard Lewis

1730s

1730 St Marks Parish formed out of St George's Parish Spotsylvania Co VA

1730 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - Thomas Dowdey trespasses on Christopher Grace. Dismissed

1731 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - Thomas Dowdey trespasses on Robert Thomas

1731 St George's Parish Levy. Thomas Dowdey "ditto ass. of" 1 "p Abraham Field" 200

1731 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - Trespass of Robert Thomas. Defendant Thomas Dowdey not being found in this county

1731 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - Road: Roleing Road from Ridge between aforemost and plentiful to Matapony River. Tythables in this room Mr. Faunteleroys Quarter 3, George Dowdeys 1, John Ward 1, Robert Anderson 1, David Bruce 1, Phillip Bush 1, Bush's son in law 1, Mr. Saunders Qr 3, Mr. Garretts Qr 3, 2 Qr of Mr. Corbin 6

1732 Spotsylvania Deed Book - John Dowdey of St Marks Parish, Spotsylvania Co VA bought 400 acres in Spotsylvania from Peter Russell wit: Mathew Stanton, Wm Morgan Sarah Russell acknowleded her dower.

1733 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - George Dowdey petitions to have his buildings works and improvments valued by 2 or more men with regard to expenses he have been at in seating 1000 acres in land in county. Granted by Jos Hawkins, Andr. Harrison, Wm Stevens, Thos. Allen.

1734 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - Alex Clinton vs George Dowdey deft. both fail to show

1734 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - John Dowdey overseer of road from Germanna to Mt. Run Bridge in the fork of Rappahannock River.

1734 Orange County Formed from Spotsylvania County, St Marks Parish now in Orange Co.

1736 March 16 Orange Co Deed Book Vol 1 pg 311 - Indenture between George Dowdey of St Marks Orange County planter to Alex Hill of same; L10; Granted to Alex Hill 200 acres in St George Parish in Spotsylvania County and in St Marks Parish Orange County part of a greater tract of 1000 acres granted to the sd George Dowdy by patent; adj William Stephens, Andrew Harrison's line, sd Dowdy's line <his "O" mark> George Dowdy; wit: John Walker, Phillip Bush, Wm Stevens; Proven 21 July 1736

1735 January 18 Orange Co Deed Book Vol 1 pg 316 - Indenture between George Dowdy of Spotsylvania planter and William Stevens of Orange County, planter; 5 shillings (for sealing) 8 shillings for sale; granted to William Stevans land containing 200 acres (no description) <his "O" mark> George Dowdy wit. Andrew Harrison, John Hieatt. Proven 21 July 1736.

1735-40, No Dowdy Court abstracts in Spotsylvania County

1739 Virginia Land Patents - Anthony Street granted 200 acres in Spotsylvania both sides of Plentiful adjacent George Dowdey, Thomas Crouthers, and Col. Gawin Corbin

1740s

1740 Aug 21 Spotsylvania Co VA DB C pg 400. Indenture between George Dowdy of St George Parish in Spotsylvania Co planter of one part and William Marsh of same of other part. L45 grant to William March a tract of land situated in St George Parish Spotsylvania Co 600 acres part of a tract of 1000 acres granted to sd George Dowdy by patent except 200 acres which sd George Dowdy sold to William Stephens and 200 acres to Alexander Hill. Bounded as follows, William Stephens and Anthony Goldon's line, Alexander Hill, Cott' Corbin's line <mark "O"> George Dowdy. Witness James Jones, John Howaton, Jos Collins; Proven 7 October 1740

1740 Virginia Land Patents - Joseph Berry granted 286 acres, 120 acres perches in Caroline County [on border?] adjacent Thacker now Prices, Dowdey, Col Wm Beverley, Baylor and Taylor. [is this James Dowdy of Caroline?] [note: Joseph Berry may be the son of William Berry who died 1720s in King William VA m. Margaret Doughty. She was the daughter of Enoch Doughty who is sometimes referred to in abstracts as Enoch "Dowdy". Family from Essex/Rappahanoch VA and documented as "Doughty" in earlier generations.

1741 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - On attachment by John Waller deft against George Dowdy the same is continued

1741 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - On attachment by John Waller gent against George Dowdey for 343 lbs tobacco, the plt having proved was due him . The court hath rated the same at 12 shillings per cent and Wm Marsh, the ganishee appearing and being sworn is ordered to pay sd Waller one pound eighteen shillings and 9 pence together with costs of this suit of the money in his lands due to the said Dowdey [note: does Dowdey work for Marsh?]

1741 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - On attachment by Wm Stevens against George Dowdey, Wm March the garnishee failing to appear when called, it is ordered that he be attached to answer next court

1741 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - Wm Marsh the garnishee being sworn declared that there was sufficient in his hands due to sd Dowdy to satisfy. Ordered that Marsh pay Stevens

1741/2 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - In action of Trespass between James Crap plaintiff and Patrick Dowdey, deft. appears on order against him and Nicholas Hawkins his security is granted plaintiff.

1750s

Oct 1750 Spotsylvania Co Court Records - Called for examination of Joseph Spenster alias Dowdy, a servant belonging to Charles Dick, gent. who is charged on suspicion of breaking open the storehouse of Fielding Lewis, gent and stealing thereat sundry pieces of gold and silver amounting to L100. Present his majesties justies John Waller, Jr, John Spotswood, Robt Jackson, Charles Dick, gentlemen.....As Joseph Spenster alias Dowdey being brought to the bar, and the court having examined and heard the witnesses is of the opinion that he is guilty in such manner that he ought to be sent to the public jail in the city of Williamsburg in order for further trial before the court of Oyer and Terminer on the 2nd Tuesday in Dec next and that the sherriff remove him as soon as conveniently may be and deliver him to the public jail keeper.
