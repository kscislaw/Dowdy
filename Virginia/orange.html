1730s

1730 St Marks Parish formed out of St George's Parish Spotsylvania Co VA

1734 Orange County VA formed from Spotsylvania County VA

1735 Orange Co VA Court Records - John Bruce vs George Dowdey (in jail)

1735 Orange Co VA Court Records - George Dowdey juror

1735 St Marks Parish Vestry Book - "to John Dowdey assignee of Wm Paxton for being Saxstone" [note: I think this is a person who looks after a particular church building]

1735 January 18 Orange Co Deed Book Vol 1 pg 316 - Indenture between George Dowdy of Spotsylvania planter and William Stevens of Orange County, planter; 5 shillings (for sealing) 8 shillings for sale; granted to William Stevans land containing 200 acres (no description) <his "O" mark> George Dowdy wit. Andrew Harrison, John Hieatt. Proven 21 July 1736.

1736 March 16 Orange Co Deed Book Vol 1 pg 311 - Indenture between George Dowdey of St Marks Orange County planter to Alex Hill of same; L10; Granted to Alex Hill 200 acres in St George Parish in Spotsylvania County and in St Marks Parish Orange County part of a greater tract of 1000 acres granted to the sd George Dowdy by patent; adj William Stephens, Andrew Harrison's line, sd Dowdy's line <his "O" mark> George Dowdy; wit: John Walker, Phillip Bush, Wm Stevens; Proven 21 July 1736

1736 Orange Co VA Court Records - John Dowdey juror

1737 Orange Co VA Court Records - Isaac Hadok tythables list: ...Jno Dowdey 1

1738 Orange Co VA Deed Book - John Dowdey of St. Marks Orange County to John Morphis 200 acres in great fork of Rappahannok River in John Dowdys upper line granted to Peter Russell 1728 and conveyed to John Dowdey in Spotsylvania 1732 wit: Jno Allen, John Cancler. [note: this land was later in Culpeper County]

1738/9 Orange Co VA Probate Records - William Hocking estate inventory. Administration by William Russell. Appraised by Thomas Pollard, Samuel Wright, John [X] Dowdee

1740s

1740 Orange Co VA Deed Book - Alex Hill of Amelia to Wm Griffith of Orange 200 acres in St George Parish and part in St Marks which Hill purchased of George Dowdey in 1736

1741 Orange Co VA Deed Book - John Morphis of Orange to Alex Cummins 200 acres in St Marks Parish...adj John Dowdey's upper line... [note this land was later in Culpeper County]

1744 Orange Co VA Court Records - Guardian Account of William Stanton. "payments to: [long list of names]...John Dowdy..."

1744 St Marks Parish Vestry Book - John Dowdey listed in St Marks Parish Vestry Book: (can't find)

1747 Orange Co VA Court Records - William Russell gent plaintiff against Thomas Dowde Deft in debt case as well by George Wythe his attorney as deft by Zachary Lewis his attorney defends the force and injury and says that he does not owe the debt in the declaration mentioned and in manner and form. Trial referred till next court.

1747 St Marks Parish Vestry Book - At a vestry for Saint mark Parish at the church for laying the Parish Levy on Monday October teh 12, 1747....Order that John Dowdy be discharged from paying parish Levy during his afflicted condition

1747 St Marks Parish Vestry Book - Levies John Dowdy "no effects" 1 poll, 7 tax

1748/9 Culpeper County formed from Orange County

1750s

1751 Virginia Land Patents - William Sherdy granted 250 acres Orange County adj James Cox, George Dowdy [land crosses Spots/Orange], Anthony Street, Thoms Gohagan, William Cox and Singleton

1752 St Marks Parish Vestry Book - At a vestry held for Saint Mark Parish at the vestry house for laying the Parish levy on Monday November the 20th 1752.......Order that the church wardens pay to John Dowdy six pound current money as soone as they receive it. <s> John Thompson, Wm Russel, Wm Green

1770s

1774 St Marks Parish Vestry Book pg 417 - At a vestry held for St Marks Parish at the glebehouse on Friday the 25th day of November 1774......"To Jacob Waller for keeping Thomas Dowdy in sickness and burial" (confirmed in the original)
