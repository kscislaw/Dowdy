James Dowdy of Craven Co NC Named as the brother of William Dowdy in the latter's 1802 Craven Co NC will. He was executor of the will. His will was probated in Craven Co NC in June 1816

1816 March 11 - Craven Co Superior Court Wills Book C pg 132 - Will of James Dowdy - State of NC Craven Co. In the name of God I James Dowdy of Craven Co being weak in body but of sound mind and memory thanks be to God for his [ ] my last will and testament in manner and form following, [religious] I devise and dispose of items in the following manner. I give unto my dear beloved son James Dowdy fifty acres of land being in the lines of Jesse Dowdy and Joseph Brothers in Allegator Point to him part to him and his heirs forever. I give to him aforesid part of a patent that that lies Gates Creek being between Pastaur Creek, William Jones lines to him and his heirs forever. I give unto my beloved son William Dowdy the plantation where I now live. I give unto my grandson Jesse Dowdy 50 acres between me and Jesse Dowdy's lies also a piece in Gates Creek [ ] 50 acres to him and heirs forever. I give unto my daughter Cloe Dowdy 50 acres of land lying between Wm Jones and Joseph Brother lands to her and her heirs forever and one feather bed . I give to my four ?right Nancy Brothers and Cloe Dowdy, Ferebee Campain, Mary Misick any household furniture, hogs and cattle to be equally divided between them four at my decease. I give my son Jesse Dowdy one broad axe and one auger and one ploy to him and heirs forever. I give unto my grandson Richard Dowdy 15 shillings to him and heirs forever. I give to my son John Dowdy on pair of iron wedges and one auger and one plane. Of all my children I granted and appoint my son William Dowdey and John Dowdy and Cloe Dowdy executors to this my last will and testament revoking all other wills etc. <s> James Dowdy Attest: Damius Lincoln, John Lincoln. Proven in court June Term 1816

married ________ She was not named in the 1816 will of James Dowdy, may have predeceased him

1(i). James Dowdy Likely the James Dowdy who was executor to the 1815 will of "cousin" Manuel Dowdy of Beaufort Co NC, inherited all the land. He was named in the 1816 will of his father James Dowdy, inheriting 50 acres at Allegator Point, plus another parcel of land around Gates Creek. He is apparently dead by 1828 when his land was partitioned and allotted to his heirs. His daughter Nancy Edward Dowdy was alloted land that was granted by will to James Dowdy Jr by his father James Dowdy Sr

1828 Craven Court Land Divisions and Plots Book - E Patrick, John Wise, Abner Brickhouse, Joshua Jones and Spencer Wise to make partition of several tracts of land lying in Craven Co viz 2 tacts containing 50 acres each lying on the head of Bear Creek adj the lands of Henry Dowdy, Elijah Dowdy and Smith Jones; also another tract estimated to contain about 70 acres lying on the North side of Bear Creek adj the lands of Smith Jones etc, heirs of William Dowdy Senior, between Hillory Dowdy, Nancy Dowdy, Cassandra Dowdy and Nancy Elizabeth Dowdy of which the last is an infant by Smith Jones her guardian heirs at law of James Dowdy dec'd. Lot 1 [description of land] alloted to Hillory Dowdy. Lot 2 [description of land] set off to Nancy Elizabeth Dowdy, minor. Lot 4 [description: containing 2 pieces which which was devised by James Dowdy Sr to his son James Dowdy Jr by his last will of 50 ac, lying on Bear Creek the other on Gates Creek] alloted to Nancy Dowdy - 18 Dec 1828

William Dowdy of Beaufort and Craven Counties NC. His will was probated April 1802 in Craven Co NC

1802 Craven Co Will Book B pg 169 - Will of William Dowdy - I William Dowdy planter of Craven County on the North side of Berry River etc. 1st I give my soul etc 2nd I give to my 2 sons Manuel and Henry Dowday a piece of land containing 125 acres lying on the East side ofthe head of Eastbeares Creek to be divided by a line the benefit of the said land to my beloved wife during her widowhood and if she marries to the two children as before mentionted. I give to my son William Dowday the plantation whereon I now live to be sold my conoe and sails to pay debts. I give what property I own after my children is raised I leave to be divided betwen my three girls namely Patty, Olivia, Sarah Dowdy. I nominate and appoint my beloved brother James Dowdy executor with my beloved wife Peggy Dowdy etc. 9th day of April 1802 <mark> William Dowdy. Wit: David Harrison, Jesse Dowdy

married Peggy ______ She was named in the 1802 will of her husband William Dowdy

1(i). Manuel Dowdy He was named in the 1802 will of his father William Dowdy, inheriting half of 125 acres following the death of William's wife Peggy; His will was probated in Beaufort Co NC in 1818

1812 Craven Co NC Apprentice Bonds -10 June 1812-[Orphans presented to the Grand Jury] Manuel Dowdy, Henry Dowdy, and William Dowdy

1815 June 5 Beaufort Co NC - Original Will of Manuel Dowdy - In the name of God amen - I Manuel Dowdy of the state of North Carolina and county Beaufort, being weak and low in health but of sound mind and memory do make this my last will and testament in manner and form following to wit - Item I give and bequeath to my Sister Sallie Dowdy of Craven County all of my hogs to her and her heirs forever. Item 2nd I give and bequeath to cousin James Dowdy all of my land lying in Craven County on the North Side of Bear Creak Run to him and his heirs forever - and lastly I nominate and appoint my cousin James Dowdy executor of this my last will and testament hereby revoking and disallowing all former wills by me made ratifying and confirming this and no other to be or contain my last will and testament - In witness whereof I have hereunto set my hand and seal this 5th of June 1818 <mark> Manuel Dowdy Wit: Benj Campen, Hillery Dowdy. Probated September Term 1818. Recorded Orphan Book A pg 290 (LDS Film # 004772508 image 414)
