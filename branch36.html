Branch #36 - Cumberland Co VA

John A. Dowdy born about 1780 (1850 census). Enumerated in Cumberland Co VA in 1840 and age 70, widowed in 1850 Cumberland Co VA census. He supposedly died November 1857 at Cumberland Co VA (need source)

23 Apr 1838. Cumberland Co. VA Court Order Book, 1835-1839, page 367: A report of the viewers of roads view a road from the parsons pines on the Ca ira Road leading to the Buckingham Road below John Spencer is returned and follows in these words and figures To Wit: We the undersigned viewers appointed by an order of the County court of Cumberland March 1838 have according to said order viewed the road mentioned in Court Order and are of opinion that the road should have the Ca Ira Road opposite John A. Dowdy's shop and intersect with the Buckingham Road at a red Oak Tree where Cloe Ellerson formerly lived that is to say, the new road should run almost entirely as the old road now runs ...
