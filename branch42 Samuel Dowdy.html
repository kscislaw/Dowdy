African American Ancestry

Samuel Dowdy of Carthage Moore Co NC; born December 1831 in North Carolina(source: 1900 census). He was age 45 (born 1835) in the 1880 census for Carthage Moore Co North Carolina, occupation farmer, both parents born in North Carolina. In the 1900 census, his father was said to have been born in North Carolina and his mother in Virginia; He was age 68 in 1900 census, married for 27 years with 11 of 14 children still alive at the time of the census. He was a farmer and rented his land at the time.

married Lucy/Leea/Lethea/Lessie Cagle about 1873 presumably in Moore County North Carolina. Her surname is confirmed by the death certificate of son George (first name listed as Althea, not Lucy) as well as the 1880 census when two young Cagle sisters, Nancy age 15 and Eliza age 12 lived with Samuel and Lucy.
