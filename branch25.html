John Dowdy of Davies Co MO born about 1790 N.C. He married in Wilkes Co NC in 1815; He was enumerated on the 1820 census for Wilkes Co NC and the 1840 census for Daviess Co MO; He died before 1842 in Davies Co MO, when his executors purchased land from his son Rufus Dowdy.

History of Santa Clara County, John moved with his parents when he was young first to Indiana, where, after residing for one year, they returned in North Carolina. The family then proceeded to Tennessee, then Missouri. In Missouri, John received his education and learned how to farm. In 1854, John crossed the plains to California, coming directly to Santa Clara county. His brother Perry also came to Santa Clara county.
