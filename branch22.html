Major Dowdy (aka surname: Dork) of Darlington Co SC born April 1843 in NC, age 35 in 1880 census for Cheraw, Chesterfield Co SC (surname: Dork); He was age 57 in Darlington SC 1900 census (surname: Dowdy)

married Mary Jane Johnson Age 25 in husband's 1880 census household (as Mollie); She was born September 1853 in SC, age 46 in 1900 census; Her surname was identified in the death certificate of daughter Alice Dowdy and delayed birth certficate of son George Dowdy
