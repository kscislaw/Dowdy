Chatham NC
Land Analysis
1763-1815

This exercise categorizes all available land records posted on this website for Chatham Co NC as located either in the Deep River Area (southern Chatham) or the Rocky River Area (northern Chatham). All references to Daniel Dowdy of Deep River and a few others are removed to concentrate on identifying the Thomas and William Dowdys of Chatham Co.

Orange Deed. Thomas Dowdy buys 50 acres from Joseph Pinson. wit John Cantril

Orange County Court. William Thompson appointed overseer of Thompson's road where it crossed Deep River with help of Thomas and William Dowdy.

  
 1774 Chatham County Court.- a Jury, or any twelve of them, appointed to review and turn the road called Husbands from where it joins Quakers Road, to poplands ford on Bair Creek & from thence to Iron Works (on Deep River) - list includes: Thomas Dowdy, William Dowdy, and others.

 1785  

William Dowdy received Grant for 212 acres, for 50 shillings per 100 acres, on Nicks Creek, John Hornaday’s line, and Wards corner. 23 Sep 1785

Thomas Dowdy received grant for 300 acres, for 50 shillings per 100 acres, on Mudlick Creek and Rocky River.
 1788  

William Ward to Chriistopher Horniday land lying on Mudlick Creek...wit: Thomas Dowdy Sr., Thomas Dowdy Jr.

Thomas Dowdy sold to Thomas Ray for 50 pounds, 90 acres on Mudlick Creek. Witnessess: David Teague & Sarah Teague
 1789    This Indenture made this 4th day of December, 1789, between Thomas Dowdy, Junior of the County of Anson, NC, planter, of the one part and Thomas Dowdy Senior of Chatham Co NC, Yoeman, of the other part. Witnesseth that the said Thomas Dowdy JR for an in consideration of the sum of 30 pounds to him in hand paid by the said Thomas Dowdy SR, doth hereby acknowledge. All that tract or parcel of land (112 acres) lying & being in the said County of Chatham, on Nicks Creek, it being part of a larger tract or parcel (212 acres) granted to William Dowdy, deceased, by a grant bearing date 23 day of September 1785, and registered in the Registers office in Book D, pg. 328-319, recourse being thereunto will more fully appear. With the said William Dowdy, dying without issue, his brother Thomas Dowdy JR, by heirship became solely entitled to the said land, etc, etc. Witnesses were Berkley Dowdy and Josiah Rogers. Proved during November session 1790 by Josiah Rogers."

 1802

Thomas Dowdy died intestate		  

Thomas Dowdy, gave “for natural love & affection of my beloved Granddaughter, Rachel Dowdy, daughter of Lucy Dowdy”100 acres on Nicks Creek, lines of John Johnson,and Barkley Dowdy, and one feather bed & furniture. Deed dated 14 Sep 1802. Witnessed by Joseph Bridges and Thomas Hardy.

Thomas Dowdy gave “for natural love and affection” to daughter Sarah Norman, wife of Wm. Norman, 100 acres on Mudlick Creek. 27 August 1802, witnessed by Edward M. Dorsey Jr & Robert Harman.
