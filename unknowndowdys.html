Pictures 1 thru 14, plus #18, were found in Eliza's trunk. Eliza was born Jul 1844 in Williamson co TN, dau of Garner Dowdy. Garner & family moved to Johnson co IL 1851 till 1869, then moved to Clay Co AR 1870. Eliza was in Clay co AR, until 1911 when she went with a niece and the nieces in-laws, to Colfax co NM where she stayed until about 1918. Niece returned to Clay co AR, but Eliza remained with niece's in-laws & went to Tucson, AZ where she died in Dec. 1920. The niece's in-laws shipped her trunk back to the niece in Clay co AR after Eliza's death.

Pictures 15-17 and 19-23 were found in the photo album of Lucy Jane Dowdy, dau of James S. Dowdy, brother to Eliza. Lucy Jane Dowdy was born Jan 1893 in Yell co AR, lived there most of her life, but died in Pulaski co AR in 1971. Album is in possession of her grandson, Wayne Hill (e-mail addr: Wjusthill@aol.com)

Notice - picture # 2 found in Eliza's trunk, and picture #17 found in Lucy Jane's album, are the same children! And the young girl on the right, in picture #13, appears to be an older version of the same little girl in #2 & #17.
