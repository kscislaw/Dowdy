Lineages
United States

These lineages last reviewed and revised, August 2023

1. Amos Dowdy of Moore Co NC and Lumpkin Co GA born 1777 likely in Moore NC. Family moved to Lumpkin GA where he died. Theorized son of Daniel Dowdy of Moore NC

2. Thomas Dowdy of Sussex VA and Chatham Co NC born about 1730; Married Lucy. Family moved to Chatham NC in the Rocky River area. Then migrated to South Carolina, Alabama, and Kentucky.

3. Daniel O'Dowdey of Currituck Co NC. Found in various early records of Currituck Co NC. He left a will there in 1765. Most of this family is yet untraced.

4. Rev. Robert Dowdy of Calhoun Co AL born about 1775 Family of Hall GA and Calhoun County AL; His large number of children are unproven; This page includes theories as to their identity. Family trees of Michael Dowdy and John Dowdy of Benton (Calhoun) County AL are presented. As well as the family tree of William Robert Dowdy of nearby Cleburne Co AL

5. Aaron Dowdy of Lumpkin Co GA born about 1796 NC; By geographic proximity, Aaron is likely the son of Daniel Dowdy of Chatham Co NC; He was a private in Capt David Gillespy's Co 4 Reg't, GA Militia, War of 1812. Died Lumpkin Co GA

6. Daniel Dowdy/Doughtie of Hertford NC; His will was probated in 1815 in Hertford Co NC, wife Winnipey and children named in the will. (Caution: he is not the Daniel Dowdy of Chatham and Moore Counties NC)

7. Josiah Dowdey, Sr. of Currituck NC; He left a will in 1828 at Currituck Co NC, naming his wife Rebecca and several children. This family is untraced beyond this date

8. William O'Dowdey of Currituck NC. He died about 1776 the year his will was probated in Currituck NC; Naming his wife and children. This page also contains several unconnected Currituck NC family trees: John Dowdy, William Dowdy, Edmund Dowdy

9. James Dowdy Sr of Moore NC born 20 April 1779; Enumerated in Moore Co NC in 1800, 1810, 1820, 1830, 1840, 1850, age 71, and in 1860 censuses, age 81; He was a member of the Friendship Baptist Church in Moore Co as well as the Bethlehem Mission Baptist Church; He died 25 December 1868 Moore NC

10. James Dowdy Sr of Goochland and Cumberland VA. born say 1720, he appeared in various court and tax records as James Dowdy Sr up through the 1770s. His children are not known, but other Dowdy families of Cumberland Co VA, including James Dowdy Jr are presented on this page. Some of this family moved to Fayette Co TN and to Saline Co MO

10a. Nephew Thomas Dowdy of Cumberland Co VA

10b. Nephew Jesse Dowdy of Cumberland Co VA

11. John Dowdy of Bedford VA born say 1740. He lived in Bedford Co VA where he appeared regularly in personal property tax lists beginning in 1782 (when lists start) through the time of his death; His will was dated 28 November 1805 naming his wife and children and was probated at Bedford Co VA 28 July 1806

12. John Dowdy of Goochland VA. He was married and his children's births were noticed in the records St James Northam Parish church in Goochland Co VA; John Dowdy died in Goochland Co VA about 1786 when his estate inventory was probated there. His male child Claiborne Dowdy is untraced.

13. Dowdy/Dowda Families of Iredell Co NC and Cherokee Co GA. This includes James A Dowdy family of Iredell Co and William A Dowdy family who migrated to Cherokee Co Georgia

14. Richard Dowdy of Cumberland and Prince Edward Co VA born about 1740 likely in Cumberland Co VA. Appeared in 1800 tax list for Prince Edward Co VA; He died in 1802 in Prince Edward County, Virginia; Several of his sons migrated to Wilkes and Ogelthorpe GA

15. Benjamin F Dowdy, Sheriff of Tattnall Co GA; born about 1816 in Screven Co GA (stated in 1850 census), his parentage is still being researched. However, he became Sheriff of Tattnall Co GA and raised a large family in that county

16. William Dowdy of Bedford VA This page includes an elder William Dowdy who married Lucinda and a younger William Dowdy "Sr" who married Elizabeth Creasy and was born about 1770 in VA; He was enumerated in Bedford Co VA census in 1830 and 1840; His will was probated in Bedford Co VA in 1844

17. William Dowdy of Hall Co GA born 13 July 1800 (tombstone), age 50 in 1850 Hall Co GA census, born NC; He died on 15 November 1880, buried in Poplar Springs Baptist Cemetery, Hall County GA (tombstone). William Dowdy's Will was recorded in Hall County. He married Amasy Rowe and had 5 daughters

18. John Dowdy Sr of Williamson Co TN.born say 1780; he was enumerated in Williamson Co TN census in 1820, 1830 and in 1840; His last will and testament was probated in Williamson Co TN in May Term of court 1845.

19. Richard Dowdy of Screven Co and Chatham Co GA, Revolutionary War Soldier was born about 1752 (according to newspaper obit). He was granted 600 acres in Screven Co GA on the Ocogee River in 1797. He moved from Screven Co GA to Savannah Chatham Co about 1823. He was taxed as a resident of Savannah in 1826, owning 850 acres in Screven Co GA, a lot in Chatham Co and 11 slaves. His will was dated 1828 naming his son, daughters and grandchildren. He died on 27 August 1832 in Savannah, Chatham Co GA, age 84

20. Benjamin F Dowdy Sr. of Fayette Co and Hardeman Co Tennessee born 7 July 1816 VA was said to have come to LaGrange, Fayette Co TN with his family in 1836. In 1839 he married Susan Akin in Fayette Co, by 1850 they were in Hardeman Co Tenn. Benjamin died 31 December 1885 buried Sauls Cemetery, Saulsbury, Hardeman Co. TN.

21. Woodson Dowdy of Franklin Co Virginia born about 1794 in VA. He married Sarah Mitchell, daughter of Robert Mitchell in 1821 at Bedford Co VA. He was married with family, enumerated in 1830 and 1840 censuses for Campbell Co VA. He migrated to Franklin Co VA by the time of the 1850 census. He died about 1860 in Franklin Co.,Va.

22. Dowdy Families of Darlington Co SC This page presents the lineages of several African American Dowdy families including Major Dowdy, Green Dowdy and George Dowdy. African American ancestry

23. Nicholas Dowdy of Chatham NC, Virginia and Greene Co Missouri born about 1825 NC. Married Lilly Edwards and was enumerated at Upper Regiment, Chatham Co NC in 1850 census, age 24 b NC; Nicholas served in the Confederate army but defected the NC Troops on 12 February 1865 near St. Petersburg, VA; Nicholas Dowdy's service record indicates at the time of his desertion he had not been paid any wages since his enlistment 24 October 1864. He lived in Carroll Co VA and then in Greene Co Missouri

24. George K Dowdy of Stoddard Co Missouri born about 1772, He moved to Kentucky abt 1810 then to Tennessee by 1814. Before his death, George K. and his sons bought and sold large pieces of property in Hickman and Fulton Cos., KY, Obion and Lake Cos., TN and New Madrid and Stoddard Cos., MO. He died there in 1852

25. John Dowdy of Daviess Co Missouri born about 1790 N.C. He married Ellander Staley in Wilkes Co NC in 1815; He was enumerated on the 1820 census for Wilkes Co NC and the 1840 census for Daviess Co MO; He died before 1842 in Davies Co MO, when his executors purchased land from his son Rufus Dowdy.

26. Walter B. Dowdy of Bedford Co VA born about 1819 VA of Bedford VA; Married with children living in the Southern District of Bedford Co VA in 1850 census, age 31; Married Mildred Swain in Bedford Co VA; Likely connected to Branch #16

27. Joseph Dowdy of Graves Co KY born about 1785 NC (1860 and 1870 census). Enumerated in Williamson Co TN in 1820 and 1830 censuses. Then migrated to Graves Co KY by the time of the 1840 census. Enumerated in Graves Co KY census for the years 1840, 1850, 1860 and 1870. He died sometime between 1870-80 at Mayfield, Graves KY. Married Mary Tucker

28. William Dowdy of Williamson Co TN and Marshall Co MS. Born say 1780 NC. Enumerated in Williamson Co TN census in 1820 and in 1830. He migrated to Marshall Co TN between 1830 and 1840 when he was enumerated in Marshall Co MS census. He was age 70 b NC in 1850 Marshall Co TN census. And age 82 in the 1860 census of the same county.

29. Thomas Dowdy of Perry Co TN born 1770 NC (1850 census age); Thomas Dowdy first appeared in Perry County in the 1820s when he was granted land and engaged surveys of that land in that county. Enumerated in Perry Co TN in 1830 but skipped in 1840 census. He was age 80 in the 1850 census of Perry County TN. He died about 1857. Married Fanny Henson

30. George W. Dowdy of Virginia, Tennessee, Mississippi, and White Co Arkansas born about 1810 in Virginia. Likely the son of Benjamin Dowdy of Powhatan Co VA who in turn was likely the son of Richard Dowdy who died in Prince Edward Co VA (Branch 14); George lived in Buckingham Co VA before moving to Fayette Tennessee at the time of the 1850 census, then DeSoto Co MS at the time of the 1860 census, then to White Co Arkansas at the time of the 1870 census.

31. William Dowdy of Stewart County Tennessee born before 1775 in South Carolina; He migrated to Stewart Co TN prior to 1807 when he was named in a county court record; He died December 1844 in Stewart Co., Tennessee, leaving a will naming 7 children

32. Orren Dowdy of Vigo Co Indiana born 11 January 1797; Married Zilpah York in Wilkes Co NC in 1822; He moved from Wilkes Co NC to Indiana after marriage; Orren Dowdy was enumerated in Vigo Co IN census in 1830, 1840, and in 1850. He died in 1851.

33. Dowdy Families of Franklin and Giles Co VA These families originated out of Franklin Co VA and are very likely the children of Ezekiel Dowdy found in Branch 11. On this page: James Dowdy and wife Elizabeth Wysong and others

34. Joe Dowdy of Richmond VA and Madison Co GA (possibly Oglethorpe Co GA) and later Jackson Co GA - According to the WPA 'Slave Narrative" biography of Doc Dowdy, his father Joe Dowdy, moved from Richmond VA with his "old" slave master to Madison Co Georgia. Bought by Noah Meadows in Madison GA; "My father was the preacher and an educated man". African American Ancestry

35. James C. Dowdy of Whitfield Co GA born about 1815 in Cherokee Territory, Georgia; Enumerated in Floyd Co GA in 1840, age 20-30; Not found in 1850 census (as yet); Enumerated at Gordon Co GA in 1860, age 45, age 55 in Whitfield Co GA census of 1870; He died after 1880 in Whitfield County GA

36. John A Dowdy of Cumberland Co Virginia born about 1780 (1850 census). Enumerated in Cumberland Co VA in 1840 and age 70, widowed in 1850 Cumberland Co VA census. He supposedly died November 1857 at Cumberland Co VA. Married Nancy Martin in Cumberland Co VA

37. Dowdy Families of Jackson Parish Louisiana This lineage page includes Thomas Dowdy born 1819, William Dowdy born 1816, and the elder Mary Dowdy (likely nee Moore) who may have been the mother of the above Thomas and William Dowdys. This lineage page also includes Congressman John V Dowdy

38. Sandy Dowdy of Chatham Co NC born about 1858 Goldston Chatham Co NC. He lived in the census household of his mother Annie Dowdy, who later married an Emerson. African American Ancestry

39. Joseph Dowdy of DeSoto Co MS born about 1819 Georgia. Family appeared in 1870 DeSoto MS census and Tate Co MS. Some family moved to Michigan. He married Martha. African American Ancestry

40. THOMAS DOWDY b abt 1738. Lived in Chatham NC around the Deep River area. Married 1) Elizabeth Parsons and 2) Rebecca. Most descendants either remained in Chatham or moved to Missouri. update pending

41. Lemuel Dowdy of Richland Co SC born 1795 in NC and died 1856. He married Emma Sawyer in 1825 in Wake Co NC. His descendants include Dr Lewis Carnegie Dowdy, Chancellor of North Carolina A&T University, among others. This family clearly originated from the early Dowdys who lived on the eastern shore of North Carolina. Both White and African American Ancestry

42. Samuel Dowdy of Moore Co NC born about 1831 (or 1835) in North Carolina. He married Lucy/Leetha Cagle about 1873 in Moore County North Carolina. His children primarily stayed in the Moore County area. African American Ancestry.

43. William Dowdy of Walker Co Alabama born 30 November 1801 in Georgia; He married Margaret Myers; He moved to Walker Co AL prior to 1838 when he patented 40 acres of land there in 1838; He died 11 December 1863; The next generation of this family moved to Texas (This lineage was formerly posted to Branch #4)

44. Obediah Dowdy of Cumberland Co and Nottoway Co VA born say 1766 likely in Cumberland Co VA where he appeared on a personal property tax list over the age of 21 in 1787. He married Mary Davenport in Cumberland Co in 1789; After marriage, he appeared in Amelia Co in the household of Mary Goode. In 1797 he purchased 115 acres of land in Nottoway Co VA where he died sometime before 1806 when his estate inventory was probated in that county.

45. Albert Jacob Dowdy of Cumberland Co VA born about 1807 in Virginia. He first appears in Cumberland Co VA records as an indentured apprentice of Anthony Crenshaw. Albert was newly married at the time of the 1830 Cumberland Co VA census. Enumerated in both the Cumberland Co VA censuses for 1840 and 1850. Age 64 in Madison Twp Cumberland Co VA (as Albert G), no children in his household. Age 72 in the 1880 census. He died 27 July 1888 in Cumberland Co VA, He married Mary Flippin

47. Dowdy Families of Craven, Beaufort and Carteret Cos NC. This page outlines the families of James Dowdy who died in Craven Co NC in 1816 and his brother William Dowdy who died in Craven Co NC in 1802

48. Dowdy Families of Campbell Co VA. Includes Stephen Dowdy (b 1792, m. Prudence Gafford), Capt Edward H Dowdy (b 1807 m. Mary Dowdy, dau of Claiborn Dowdy); Wyatt Dowdy (b 1813 m. Sarah Clark); Lewis C Dowdy (b 1808 m. Mildred Moore)

49. John Dowdy of Tuscaloosa AL and Chickasaw MS. born about 1801 in South Carolina (1850 census info). If the place of birth is correct, then he is likely either the son of George Dowdy of Chesterfield Co SC or instead Thomas Dowdy of the same place. He married Francis Phillips in Tuscaloosa Co AL in 1825

50. Dowdy Families of Monroe Co WV and Gallia Co OH. Includes Edward Dowdy born in Bedford Co Va in 1825 (married Elizabeth Clark); Wyatt Dowdy born about 1824 VA (married Eliz Wood and Martha Noel) and David Dowdy born about 1816 likely in Bedford Co VA (married Eliz Cheatwood in Bedford Co VA and Nancy Rife in Gallia OH)
