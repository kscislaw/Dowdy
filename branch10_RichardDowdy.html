Richard Dowdy of Cumberland VA and Fayette Co TN born 16 May 1787 presumably in Cumberland Co VA; Named in the 1804 will of father James Dowdy Jr. He married and moved to Fayette Co Tennessee; He died in Fayette Co TN sometime before 1843 when Wm P Dowdy appeared in Fayette Co TN records acting as administrator

November 1810 In Chancery Thomas Dowdy and Jesse Dowdy, Executors of James Dowdy, deceased, (Plaintiffs) against: The following children and representatives of James Dowdy, deceased, to wit, Richard Dowdy, James Dowdy, Jesse Dowdy, Jane Dowdy, Sally Dowdy, Polly Dowdy by Richard Dowdy her guardian, William Dowdy, an infant child and only of Elizabeth Dowdy, daughter of James Dowdy, deceased, by Richard Dowdy his guardian (defendants):

1811 Cumberland County Deed - This Indenture 7 November 1811 between Frances Dowdy relict of James Dowdy, deceased, Richard Dowdy, James Dowdy, and Jesse Dowdy, sons of said deceased, all of the county of Cumberland of the one part and Jacob Bransford of the same county of the other part... for the consideration of the sum of seven hundred eighty two dollars thirty four cents ... a certain tract of land lying in the aforesaid county of Cumberland, containing two hundred twenty nine acres be the same more or less, and bounded by the lands of the said Jacob Bransford, John Colquitt, Noton Goodman, little Guinea creek and the land of Lewis Isbell, it being the same the said James Dowdy, deceased, possessed of and where on the said Frances now resides

13 October 1815 [record annexed to Chancery suit dated 1848) - I have this day received of my mother by and with the consent of the legatees of my father James Dowdy dec'd Jack a negro fellow willed to me by my father to taken at the death of my mother in consequence of my recieving him at this time I reliinquish th ewhole of my interest and claim to the said estate as witness my hand and seal this 13 Oct 1815 <s> Richard Dowdy Test: Jno Spencer

[Note: the crux of later Chancery claims by Richard Dowdy e.g. document dated 15 Feb 1836 is that he is entitled by law to an equal share of property inherited by sister Elizabeth and brother Jesse from the 1804 will of their father James Dowdy, both children Richard claims died intestate and without heirs (His claim is proven to be in error w.r.t. Elizabeth but not Jesse)]

Fayette Co TN Will Book A pg 406 - Will of Martha Dowdy - I Martha Dowdy considering the uncertainty of this mortal life and being of sound mind and memory do make and publish this my last will and testament manner and form following. That is to say After all of my just debts are paid I give and bequeath unto my children Mary Dowdy, Elizabeth Dowdy, William P Dowdy, Mahaly Munroe and the heirs of Manda C Robertson all the right and interest that I have in the tract of land that I know reside an containing by estimation one hundred and seventy two and 1/2 acres - to be equally divided but my daughter Mary is to have preference of the dwelling house. I further give and bequeath unto my daughter two beds and steads and furniture and one bureau one safe and my table ware the balance of my personal property to be sold and the proceeds equally divided. I also give unto Jacob Bowels one dollar in money. I hereby appoint my son Wm P Dowdy sole executor of this my last will and testament in lifetime. I hereby set my hand and seal this the 21st day of October one thousand eight hundred and fifty <X> Martha Dowdy; witness G M Young, David W Monroe; Proven December Term 1850
