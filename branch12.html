John Dowdy of Goochland Co Virginia He was married and his children's births were noticed in the records St James Northam Parish church in Goochland Co VA; John Dowdy died in Goochland Co VA about 1786 when his estate inventory was probated there (Bk 14 page 347 Goochland VA)

1759 Goochland Co VA December Court (OB 8):Wm Eaves comes into court and makes oath that he verily believes his life in danger by John Dowdy and that he is apprehensive the said John will and his wife an injury in some respect or other. Whereupon the said John not appearing make any defense it is ordered by the court that the sherriff take him into custody and safely keep him until he enter into recognizance in the said 20 pounds with two dood and sufficient securities each in the sum of 10 pounds payable to our soverign lord the king his heirs and successors for his dood behavior for 1 year and a day towards his majesty and his successors Leige people and more especially to the said William of ~ christian [??? that is what it said] his wife and in failure that he be kept in custody til the term aforesaid be fully expired

1763 Goochland Co VA May Court (OB 8): Chas Woodson admin of Jacob Woodson, dec'd vs. John Dowdy and Joseph Johnson defts. The summons and copy pettitions beign returned executed the deft John appears in his proper person and agrees to pay the plt his costs. Whereupon on the motion of the petitioner aforesaid be dismissed and that the petitioner recover agst the deft his costs by him in this behalf expended. Cost 56 lbs tobacco.

1776 Goochland Co VA List of Tithables - John Dowdy 1 tithe

1786 Goochland Co VA Will Book 14 pg 347 - John Dowdy Estate Inventory

1787 and 1788 (appears on both DB 15 pg 516,520) List of insolvents in the lower part of Goochland Co returned by Reuben Turner collector for Wm Roysten late Sherriff for the year 1787 (and 1788) . ...[list]...John Dowdy dec'd, 122 acres, tax 0 13 9

1789, 1790, 1791, 1792, 1793, 1794 Goochland Co VA Personal Property Tax Lists - John Dowdy, dec'd estate 122 acres

married Elizabeth Hubbard on 7 January 1762 at St James Northam Parish Goochland VA [source: Douglas Register pg6]

1787 Goochland Co Va Personal Property Tax List - Elizabeth Dowdy 00002 [not tithable]

children:

1(i). Frances Dowdy born 19 December 1762 at St James Northam Parish Goochland VA; She married William Layne on 22 July 1786 at Goochland Co VA and they moved to Tennessee; She died in Williamson Co TN in 1837

1(ii). Clayburn Dowdy born 4 March 1765 at St. James Northam Parish, Goochland, Virginia; He is possibly the same Claiborne Dowdy who was awarded land in Kentucky for service in the Revolutionary War. The land grant document was dated 1784 when this Claiborne Dowdy would be approximately 19 years old - thus some doubt if he is the same son of John Dowdy of Goochland Co VA [see revolutionary land warrant image]

1783 April 15 - Revolutionary War Service Record - A list of soldiers of the Virginia Line on Continental establishment who have receieved certficates for the balance of their full pay - L36 issued to Ambrose Dowdy, soldier. Virginia

1785 - Early Virginia Religious Petitions - November 2, 1785, Goochland, Against assessment bill. [petitioners] - Claiborne Dowdy
