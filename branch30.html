George W. Dowdy
Branch #30

This family appears to be part of the Dowdys of Prince Edward Co VA > to Powhatan > to Buckingham > to Tennessee > to Mississippi > to Arkansas

This Lineage Last Reviewed and Updated September 2023
George W Dowdy likely the son of Benjamin Dowdy of Powhatan Co VA who in turn was likely the son of Richard Dowdy who died in Prince Edward Co VA; He lived in Buckingham Co VA before moving to Fayette Tennessee at the time of the 1850 census, then DeSoto Co MS at the time of the 1860 census, then to White Co Arkansas at the time of the 1870 census.
