James Dowdy born about 1799 in VA, age 50 in 1850 census; He first appeared in records as being of tithable age in 1819 Franklin County personal property tax lists; He married in Franklin County VA earlier in that year. He and wife and one daughter appeared in Franklin Co census in 1820. The family next moved to Giles County VA where James was enumerated there in 1830 and in 1840 censuses. In 1850, James was identified as age 50, farmer, born Virginia. His household included his wife Elizabeth and numerous children born after 1829. James Dowdy died sometime soon after 1850 census, documents regarding the disposition of his estate in Chancery Court are dated early 1851
