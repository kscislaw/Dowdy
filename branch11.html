John Dowdy Sr. of Bedford Co Virginia born say 1740. He lived in Bedford Co VA where he appeared regularly in personal property tax lists beginning in 1782 (when lists start) through the time of his death; His will was dated 28 November 1805 naming his wife and children and was probated at Bedford Co VA 28 July 1806 (Bk 3/192)
