2. Lucy DOWDY; born about 1780; died about 1835 Hamilton County, OH.

Chatham NC Deed Book O, pg. 299Thomas Dowdy ( II ), George Dowdy, Barkley Dowdy, Edward Bray (husband of Anne Dowdy), Augustin Tyser (husband of Lucy Dowdy) & William Dowdy, rightful heirs of Thomas Dowdy, deceased, in consequence of Thomas Dowdy dying intestate, sold to William Norman (husband of Sarah Dowdy) for 52 pounds, 10 shillings, 182 acres on Rockey River, Normans present corner, Brays corner, Bridges line, and Rachel Dowdys line. Witnesses Thomas Hadley, Joshua Hadley, Wm. Lewis, Hannah Hobson. Dated the 1 Mar 1806. Proven February session 1807 by oath of Thomas Hadley & Hannah Hobson

married Augustine TICER. Moved to Hamilton Ohio. Living in Hamilton County Ohio in 1824 when as attorney for Rachel Dowdy, he sold her land in Chatham County NC

1824 Deed Bk AA. Augustine Tysor and Lucy Tysor of Hamilton Co OH attorneys for Rachel Dowdy of same place to William Dowdy of Chatham Co NC, tract in Chatham Co on Nixes Creek adj John Johnson, Horace D. Bridges adn ?Isham Pussor, 100 ac. it being a tract of land conveyed from Thomas Dowdy to the said Rachel Dowdy.

3. Rachel DOWDY daughter born to Lucy "Dowdy" inherited land from her grandfather Thomas Dowdy of Rocky River Chatham NC. The land was sold by her parents as "attorneys" to William Dowdy of Chatham County in 1824. The family was living in Hamilton County Ohio at the time.

Chatham NC Deed Book N, pg. 3 Thomas Dowdy, gave “for natural love & affection of my beloved Granddaughter, Rachel Dowdy, daughter of Lucy Dowdy”100 acres on Nicks Creek, lines of John Johnson,and Barkley Dowdy, and one feather bed & furniture. Deed dated 14 Sep 1802. Witnessed by Joseph Bridges and Thomas Hardy. Proven November session 1802 by Joseph Bridges

3. Uriah TICER born on 9 Dec 1808 in Cincinnati, Hamilton, Ohio and d Oct 7, 1894 in Emporia, Lyon, Kansas.

married Mary Ann GLISSON Nov 29 1835 in Hamilton County OH.

3. Jennie TICER b Dec 23, 1803.

married Andrew VOSS on January of 1819 in Hamilton Co OH (Book 1 pg 400) and moved to Indiana


Editors note: According to the approved DAR application dated 1939, this Jennie Ticer is the daughter of a Lucy Dowdy and Augustine Ticer. However, the application claims that this particular Lucy Dowdy was the daughter of a William Dowdy who was killed by Tories in Chatham County NC, and his wife Elizabeth Seabrook Bartlett. This DAR application was given the number #315463 and cited application #268032 and Son's of the Revolution papers of Dr. Voss Harrell, Samuel Runnells Harrell, and Maurice Ticer Harrell. The website editor has long believed these DAR applications contained flawed lineages. For example, the heir at law of William Dowdy (killed by Tories) was his brother Thomas Dowdy Jr. He had no wife or children.
