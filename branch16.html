William Dowdy of Bedford Co VA born say 1750; Likely originated in Cumberland Co VA, but settled and died in Bedford County VA; His death and estate administration was mentioned in the course of a large lawsuit William Dowdy originated in Cumberland Co against Harrison Jones. Hundley Dowdy filed in Bedford Co as adminsitrator of William Dowdy's estate in continuation of the lawsuit against Harrison Jones

At a District Court held at Prince Edward Court house the 12th day of April 1796 Harrison Jones and James Deane against Hundley Dowdy Appellant on an appeal Hundley Dowdy Administrator of William Dowdy deceased appeared then a Judgment of the Court of Cumberland County recovered by the said William Dowdy in his lifetime the 28th day of October 1794 for five hundred and sixty nine pounds of Toban also Twenty six pounds ten shillings and nine pence with lawful Interest thereon from the 9th day of February 1793 till paid and Cost This day came the parties by their Counsel , and thereupon the Transcript of the Record of the Judgment aforesaid being seen and inspected it sums to the Court that the said Judgment is mous in this that the Judgment is for the amount of the Condition only , and and for the penally of the land . Therefore it is considered that the same be reversed and annulled , and that the Appellants recover against the apper their Costs by them expended in presenting their appeal aforesaid here , as also their costs about their defence expended in the County Court aforesaidand this Court proceeding to give such Judgement as the said County Court ought to have given It is considered by the Court that the Appeller recover against the Appellants one Thousand one hundred and thirty eight pounds of Tobacco also Fifty three pounds one shilling and six the penally of the fourth coming time , in the Record of Appeal aforesaid mentioned , together with his least in this behalf of pended But this Judgment is the discharged by the payment of Five hundred and sixty nine pounds of land , also twenty six pounds ten shillings and impence with Interest thereon at five per Centum per annum from February 9th 1793 ill payment and the Cost
