1(iii). John Dowdy born 24 April 1762 Albemarle Parish, Sussex Co., VA. John was christened 22 Jun 1762 by Rev. Willis. His sponsors were Nathan Northington, John Sands and Martha Seaborn. John Dowdy's estate was administered in Chatham County NC in 1792. John was not listed in the list of rightful heirs of Thomas Dowdy in the 1806 sale of his land.

1792 Loose Papers - NC - Chatham Co (FHC film #1065099) Inventory of John Dowdy’s estate filed in court by Administrators John & Hannah Terry. Consisted only of 1 mare & colt, 2 cows, 1 sow, misc.dishes, pans & farming tools, and one saddle. May Term of court – 1792

married Hannah Whitehead; born 1765 in Caswell, NC; daughter of Josiah Whitehead; She married 2nd, James Terry; Hannah Terry's will, dated 11 Apr 1835, mentions her daughter Sarah, son William Dowdy and grandson Hiram Dowdy. Hannah Terry's daughter Catherine Terry married her mother Hannah Terry's nephew, James Dowdy of Lauderdale Co AL., a pensioner of the War of 1812.

1835 April 11 - Chatham Co WB C pg 190 - Will of Hannah Terry. My grandson William Dowdy. My grandson Hiram Dowdy when he arrives at age 21 <mark> Hannah Terry. Test: Jno S Guthrie, John Whitehead. Will was presented by Jno S Dowdy whereupon Joseph Holoday entered into bond and administration with the will annexed with Stephen Chamness and Joseph Dowdy as securities.

children:

2(i). Sarah [Dowdy] mentioned as daughter of Hannah Terry in her 1835 will. May not be a Dowdy.

2(ii). William Dowdy born 1784 to 1790; was given 93 acres of land by his wife's grandfather. William Dowdy's step-father James Terry witnessed the deed and proved it in court; died between 1840 and 1850. He was represented in the 1806 land sale as the heir of Thomas Dowdy, Sr's son John Dowdy who died in 1792. Note: John's heirs would need to be represented in the 1806 sale of Thomas' land: He was deceased by the time of the 1850 census when his wife Rebecca was enumerated in the census household of her children

Chatham NC Deed Book O, pg. 299 Thomas Dowdy, George Dowdy, Barkley Dowdy, Edward Bray (husband of Anne Dowdy), Augustin Tyser (husband of Lucy Dowdy) & William Dowdy, rightful heirs of Thomas Dowdy, deceased, in consequence of Thomas Dowdy dying intestate, sold to William Norman (husband of Sarah Dowdy) for 52 pounds, 10 shillings, 182 acres on Rockey River, Normans present corner, Brays corner, Bridges line, and Rachel Dowdys line. Witnesses Thomas Hadley, Joshua Hadley, Wm. Lewis, Hannah Hobson. Dated the 1 Mar 1806. Proven February session 1807 by oath of Thomas Hadley & Hannah Hobson
