John Dowdy
Branch #49 - Chickasaw Co MS

This Lineage Last Reviewed on November 2023
John Dowdy of Tuscaloosa Co AL and Chickasaw Co MS; born about 1801 in South Carolina (1850 census info). If the place of birth is correct, then he is likely either the son of George Dowdy of Chesterfield Co SC or instead Thomas Dowdy of the same place. Both families originated out of the Rocky River area of Chatham Co NC. John Dowdy first appears in records in 1825 when he married Francis Phillips in Tuscaloosa Co AL. He next appears in the same county in the 1830 census. In the 1830, John Dowdy appeared in marriage records as a Justice of the Peace. He began to obtain BLM land warrants in Tuscaloosa Co in 1834 and continued obtaining warrants throughout the 1830s. His land was adjacent Wyatt Poe and John Poe, names originating out of Chatham Co NC. He was enumerated in the 1840 Tuscaloosa Co census, age 40-50, born 1790-1800). John Dowdy appeared in various Tuscaloosa County deeds and court records through the 1840s. He moved to Chickasaw Co MS by the time of the 1850 census where he was enumerated there with wife and family, age 49 born South Carolina. This enumeration district later fell within the border of Calhoun Co MS; He must have died sometime prior to the 1860 census, when several of his adult unmarried children were living in a household together.
