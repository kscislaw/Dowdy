Walter B. Dowdy
Branch #26

This Lineage Last Reviewed and Updated August 2023
Walter B. Dowdy of Bedford Co VA; born about 1819 VA of Bedford VA; Walter B Dowdy was married with children living in the Southern District of Bedford Co VA in 1850 census, age 31; indicating In September 1855, Samuel H Quarles secured the administrative bond on the estate of Walter B Dowdy in Bedford Co VA who was in turn then described as a 'lunatic'. Enumerated at Davis Mills PO in Bedford VA in 1860 census, age 42, farmer without his wife Mildred Dowdy; Living adjacent to William Dowdy (age 46); Younger children were scattered in the 1870 census, He was identified in the 1854 will of Anna Creasey of Bedford Co VA as her nephew. Walter was deemed non-compus mentis during his administration of Anna Creasy's estate. (Anna Creasey was age 70 in 1850 census, unsure if she was a spinster). He is likely the son of Hundley Dowdy. See the 1855 deed of gift to widow Mildred and her children, likely upon the death of husband Walter B Dowdy; See Branch 16

1841 December 15 - Bedford Co DB 29 pg 311 - Lewis Overstreet and wife Mary Ann of Bedford Co to Walter B Dowdy of said Co $150; Tract of land lying on south side of Dumpling Mountain containing 75 acres adj lands of John B Will', Hundley Dowdy Sr and others <s> Lewis Overstreet, <mark> Mary Ann Overstreet; Acknowledge in Bedford court 15 December 1841

Walter B. Dowdy is listed as a witness to the will of William Dowdy (d. 1844) of Bedford and as a surety to the marriage of Hundley Dowdy's daughter, Locky, in 1846.

1846 February 19 - Bedford Co DB 32 pg 269 - Isaac Drewry of 1st part. Walter B Dowdy of 2nd part . William S. Dowdy of 3rd part. Isaac Drewry is bound to the sd William S Dowdy in sum of $27 (dated 5 Nov 1845) Security. Sold unto Walter B Dowdy following to wit: mare bought off sd W B Dowdy. Should Drewry default. <s> Isaac Drury. Acknowledged Bedford Court 21 Feb 1846

1850 Bedford Co VA Census - Walter B Dowdy age 31 VA farmer; Mildred F age 21 VA; John P age 4 VA; Ann M age 3 VA; Martha J age 1 VA

1854 February 6 Bedford Co VA Will Book - Will of Anna Creasy - I desire that my friend and nephew , Walter B Dowdy shall have all the residue of my property both real and personal of whatever description Third I constitute & appoint my Nephew Walter B Dowdy my executor (no other relatives named in the Will)

1830 Bedford Co Census Anna Creasy [no males] - age 30-40 (born 1790-1800 (possibly the aunt of Walter B Dowdy, named daughter of John Creasy who died 1825 in Bedford VA; Not found in 1840; Age 70 in 1850 census (b 1780); If spinster dau of John Creasy, then her brother in laws were William S Dowdy and Hundley Dowdy - thus son of one of those two Dowdys.

1854 February - Bedford Circuit Court - ....Walter B Dowdy was admitted into the lunatic asylum for 16 days....

1855 Sept 11 - Bedford Co Superior Court Probate Bonds pg 169 - I Samuel H Qualres am held and firmly bound unto the Commonwealth of Virginia $300. The condition of the above obligation is such that Samuel H Quarles has been appointed a committee to take charge of the estate of Walter B Dowdy, a lunatic, fathfully discharge duties according to the law

1860 Bedford Co VA Census - W. B. Dowdy 42 Farmer; Jnor 14; A. M. (female) 12; M. J. (female) 10; Wm. 8; P. F. (male) 6; E. J. (male) 4

married Mildred Swain on 4 March 1844 in Bedford Co VA; She was age 21 in 1850 census; She is not found in her husband's 1860 census household

Bedford VA Marriage Bonds - Mar. 4, 1844; Walter B. Dowdy & Mildred Swain; Calohill M. Swain, Surety; Consent of Nancy Swain.

Bedford DB 37 pg 470 - 9 July 1855 - Hundly Dowdy Sr to Mildred T Dowdy and her children for love and affection for sd Mildren Dowdy and her children all his land it being the same wheron he now resides consisting of several contiguous tracts containing altogether 200 acres near the Dumplin [ ] and bounded by the lands of Cary A Dowdy, John Bawy..[] Overstreet at aothers.
