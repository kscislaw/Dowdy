Thomas Dowdy
Branch #29 - Perry County TN

Warning: There seems to be some confusion about this Thomas Dowdy being the same person as Thomas Dowdy of Chatham NC who died 1859 and whose wife was Sarah ?Riddle. It has been stated in some research that Thomas Dowdy of Chatham was buried in Perry Co TN. However, both Thomas Dowdys appear in Chatham NC and Perry TN 1850 censuses. They cannot be the same person.

This Lineage Last Reviewed and Updated September 2023
Thomas Dowdy of Perry County Tennessee born 1770 NC (1850 census age); Thomas Dowdy first appeared in Perry County in the 1820s when he was granted land and engaged surveys of that land in that county. Enumerated in Perry Co TN in 1830 but skipped in 1840 census. He was age 80 in the 1850 census of Perry County TN. He died about 1857.
