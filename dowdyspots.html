HOWELL DAWDY GENEALOGY

This family has been listed in abstracts as both "Dawdy" and "Dowdy". However, it seems that "Dawdy" is the correct spelling. This family appears to be separate from any other "Dowdy" lineage, although their migration followed other "Dowdy" migrations.

Source: The following family group sheet was submitted to the LDS Ancestry File by Marilyn Carson of Placenta CA

Posting on the Ancestry.com surname board for "Dawdy"

"My gosh!! I could hardly believe my eyes when I visited Ancestry.com today and saw how the Dawdy contingency had multiplied over-night. Terrific!!

To the best of my knowledge, all Dawdys in the USA and Canada go back to 2 brothers in NJ, James born there c 1736 and Howell or Hoel born iether NJ or the UK c 1742-46.

There are other Dawdys of about the same time frame but no connection to James and Howell has been established yet; Antice Dawdy died intestate in Maryland; Obadiah also said to have been Rev. war soldier; Abraham Dawdie who came from Rotterdam 1754 to Philadelphia on the ship Nancey. May also be a Michael from VA.

Almost without exception, Howell Dawdy and his descendants stayed in the US; brother James Dawdy also stayed in the US and is buried in Hope, NJ. All but his youngest son, Wilson, came to Ontario,Canada iether c 1791 or 1811 depending on who is talking. Jeremiah was a son of James Dawdy and both Howell and James had sons named James. It is thought their father may have been named Jacob but nothing has been put forth to substantiate -- however,a Jacob Dohotey is to be found in norhtern NJ census as a posibility given the variety of spellings of Dawdy.

Hope this helps -- contact me if I can answer any further questions or just to compare notes. Thanx, Joan Dawdy Wilton"
