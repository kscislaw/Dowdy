Thomas Dowdy of Deep River, Chatham County NC

 

Thomas Dowdy - born about 1738. Lived in the Deep River area of Chatham Co NC. LW&T dated 27 January 1805. Probated 1815

CHATHAM COUNTY, NC - WILLS - Thomas Dowdy, Sr., 27 Jan 1805 - Source - Chatham County, N.C. Wills, Vol. A, (1798-1819) pg. 273, 274

In the name of God, Amen. I, Thomas Dowdy, Sen'r. being in perfect senses and memory, I constitute this my last (repeats my last) will and testament in the following manner, to wit. In the first place, I give and bequeath my soul into the hands of the Almighty God who gave it, and my body to be buried in a Christian & decent manner. I then dispose of my property in the following manner. I give unto my daughter Nelly Goodman, one dollar, I also, give unto my daughter Lucy Dowdy, one dollar. Item, I give unto my daughter Sarah Dowdy, one dollar. Item, I give unto my son John Dowdy, one dollar. Item, I give unto my son William Dowdy, one dollar. Item, I give unto my son Thomas Dowdy, one dollar. Item, I give unto my son James Dowdy, one dollar. Item, I give unto my son Armstead Dowdy, one dollar. Item, I give unto my son Joab Dowdy, one dollar, one mare, bed and furniture. Item, I give unto my son Benjamin Dowdy, one horse, bed and furniture. Item, I give unto my son Balaam Dowdy, one dollar. Item, I give unto my daughter Rebecca Dowdy, one mare, bed and furniture. All the rest of my property, consisting of land and tenements, and all my household and kitchen furniture, I give unto my beloved wife Rebecca Dowdy, (during ?) her lifetime, and after her death, it is my desire that it should all be equally divided amongst the following persons, Thomas Dowdy, James Dowdy, Armstead Dowdy, Balaam Dowdy, Joab Dowdy, Benjamin Dowdy, and Rebecca Dowdy. The above I constitute my last will and testament, this 27th of January in the year of our lord one thousand eight hundred & five. Test: John McIntosh Chiles McGee Thomas (x) Dowdy

Proved February session 1815, by John McIntosh
