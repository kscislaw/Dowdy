2. Benjamin DOWDY born abt 1780 in Chatham County NC [date is from 1850 Henderson TN census- age 70 b NC]; moved to Henderson TN

Benjamin moved to North Georgia with his brothers and cousins. In 1810 he purchased 50 acres of land in Jackson County. He appears as a tax defaulter in 1818 and 1820 next door Franklin County GA and appears in the 1820 Jackson County census with 2 sons age 0-10.

Deed Book X, pg. 271 Power of Attorney given to Thomas Dowdy by his brothers James Dowdy, Armstead Dowdy Balaam Dowdy & Benjamin Dowdy of Franklin co. Georgia, to sell their land they inherited from their father, Thomas Dowdy (II). Witnesses Samuel Mosby Sr, James Dowdy & Benjamin Dowdy. 24 Nov 1820. Proven during Chatham Co NC May Session 1821 by oath of Benjamin Dowdy.

However, he may have returned to Chatham County for a few years after 1820, but may be the Benjamin Dowdy in Henry County GA in 1830. 1830 Henry Co GA Census: Benjamin Dowdy and wife both age 30-40; 2 sons (10-15 and under 5), and 1 daughter (age 5-10). Distant cousin Aaron Dowdy (of the Moore Co NC Dowdys) lived nearby

Benjamin moved to Henderson Co TN before 1840, appearing in that census age 50-60 with 3 sons viz: ages 0-5, 5-10, and 10-15; with 3 daughters viz: 0-5 and 2 aged 5-10. His wife was age 30-40. She apparently died before 1850.

Note: Another possibly distantly related Amos Dowdy family migrated from Marshall TN to Henderson TN in the 1860 census
