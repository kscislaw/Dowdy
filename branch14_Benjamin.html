Evidence for making link from Benjamin Dowdy, son of Richard Dowdy who died Prince Edward Co VA to the following family: 1) there are simply no other Benjamin Dowdys of age in this era. The following Benjamin Dowdy is alive and active in records in 1828 just like Benjamin the son of Richard Dowdy dec'd 2) The following Benjamin Dowdy resideds in nearby counties to Prince Edward Co. His children migrated to the same southern counties as some of Richard Dowdy's other's descendants (cousins). The migration makes sense.
