2.i William Dowdy b abt 1755. Killed by Tories 1782

1782 April William Dowdy killed by Tories. Report of Col David Fanning: "...I had set out for Chatham, when I learned a wedding was to be that day. On my way I took one prisoner before I came to the house. Ther being but five of us, we immediately surrounded the house in full charge. I ordered them immediately out of the house; three of my men went into the house and drove them all out, one by one; I caused them all to stand in a row to examine them, to see if I knew any of them that was bad men. I found one, by the name of William Dowdy, concealed upstairs. One of my men fired at him as he was running from one house to the other. He received the ball in the shoulder. I then, having my pistols in hands, discharged them both at his breast, with which he fell, and that night expired. I then parolled the rest, on the 25th." (Source R.M. Smith)
